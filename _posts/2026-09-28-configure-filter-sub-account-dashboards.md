If you manage multiple client sub-accounts in HighLevel, you have likely run into the universal agency reporting headache: a default dashboard that shows everyone's numbers all at once, forcing your clients to mentally filter out data that doesn't apply to them.

HighLevel has rolled out a major update to general widgets in sub-account dashboards that solves this cleanly. You can now configure, permanently filter, and customize individual widgets without relying on clunky workarounds or forcing clients to manually slice data every time they log in.

### What You Can Now Do

Previously, general widgets offered a one-size-fits-all view of the sub-account. With this update, editing a widget opens up three distinct tabs: Configure, Conditions, and Themes.

*   **Permanent Data Filters:** Under the new Conditions tab, you can lock a widget to specific criteria. Set a filter for `Assigned user = Logged in user`, and every sales rep or team member who opens the dashboard instantly sees only their own pipeline performance. You can also filter by tags, lead values, attribution sources, and custom fields.
*   **Date Range Overrides:** You no longer have to rely solely on the global dashboard date filter. You can lock an individual widget to a specific time frame—such as locking a high-level KPI card to "This Year" with a year-over-year comparison against last year.
*   **Explicit Widget Faces:** HighLevel has added the active date property and date range directly onto the face of the widget. This eliminates the confusion caused by two similar metrics showing different numbers because they were pulling from conflicting date windows.
*   **White-Label Polish:** You can now rename widgets to match client-friendly terminology, apply visual color themes that match your branding, and duplicate widgets to showcase different audience segments.

### A Practical Agency Use Case

Imagine you run marketing for a local home services company with three distinct dispatchers. In the past, creating a leaderboard meant building custom reports outside the standard dashboard.

Now, you can drop three general opportunity widgets onto the sub-account dashboard, applying a permanent condition to each one for a specific assigned user. When Dispatcher A logs in, they see their closed-won metrics instantly. When the business owner logs in, they see the aggregated view. You spend zero time building separate views, and the client gets a clean, professional interface that feels custom-coded for their business.

Head over to any sub-account dashboard, click **Edit dashboard** in the top right corner, and click the three-dot menu on any general widget to start configuring your conditions, date ranges, and custom titles. These configurations persist natively, meaning your custom segments stay intact every single time a user opens the page.
