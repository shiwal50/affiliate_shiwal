If you manage high-volume inbound leads for clients, you know the frustration of relying solely on random split paths in automation workflows. Setting a 50/50 percentage split on paper never quite translates to an even distribution in practice—especially when lead volume fluctuates or sample sizes are small. HighLevel has quietly rolled out a massive upgrade to workflow routing that fixes this exact problem: the addition of Even and Weighted splits.

Previously, agencies trying to distribute leads evenly across multiple sales reps, follow-up sequences, or testing variations had to settle for random assignment. Over a hundred or a thousand leads, randomness often clumps up, leaving some reps overwhelmed while others sit idle. 

### How Even and Weighted Splits Change the Game

The update introduces two distinct distribution types to the standard Split action inside your workflows:

* **Even Split:** Automatically rotates incoming contacts down each available path in a strict turn-based sequence. If you have two paths, it alternates one-to-one. If you have three, it cycles through them cleanly. This guarantees balanced distribution for team lead-sharing or A/B testing variations.
* **Weighted Split:** Gives you precise percentage-based control using whole numbers that total 100%, but applies them in a turn-based manner rather than a pure coin-flip probability. If you set a 60/40 split, the system sends three out of every five contacts down the primary path and two down the secondary. 

For agency owners running internal lead generation or building complex client operations, this means true capacity-based routing. If one sales closer can handle heavy volume while a junior rep takes a smaller slice, you can finally mirror that exact capacity without writing custom code or relying on external middleware like Zapier.

### Practical Agency Implementation

Imagine you are setting up an aggressive outbound appointment-setting campaign for a local service client. You want to test two completely different SMS opening hooks to see which one books more calls. 

In the past, a random split might send 30 leads to Path A and 70 leads to Path B simply due to variance, skewing your metrics and forcing you to wait longer for statistical significance. With the new Even split configuration, both messaging variations receive an identical, locked-in number of interactions right out of the gate. 

Similarly, if you are distributing inbound leads among a client's sales team based on their closing tiers, the Weighted split lets you allocate exact ratios reliably. Once contacts enter the split, their path is locked in. If a contact re-enters the workflow later, you can configure them to keep the same path, maintaining consistency in their customer journey.
