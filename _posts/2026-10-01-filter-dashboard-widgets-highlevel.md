For years, agency owners running HighLevel sub-accounts have faced a minor but persistent client frustration: the main dashboard is too noisy. When a client logs in, they see a macro view of the entire account—every lead, every pipeline stage, and every team member's activity. If you manage a sales floor with five reps, your client doesn't necessarily want to see the aggregate total on day one; they want to see their own metrics, or a specific slice of data tailored to their login.

HighLevel has finally solved this with a major upgrade to sub-account general widgets. You can now configure, filter, and isolate data directly inside individual dashboard widgets, turning a generic reporting screen into a clean, client-ready display.

### What's Actually New in Widget Configuration

Instead of accepting the default aggregate view, you can now hit 'Edit dashboard', open a general widget's settings, and apply permanent rules. This update introduces three core layers of control:

1. **Permanent Conditions:** You can filter a widget by assigned user, tags, source, lead value, custom fields, and attribution. If you set the assigned user to the currently logged-in user, every staff member who opens that dashboard automatically sees only their own assigned metrics. No more building duplicate custom dashboards for every single rep.

2. **Date Range Overrides:** Widgets are no longer strictly chained to the global dashboard date filter. You can lock a specific widget to 'This Year' while the rest of the page looks at 'This Month', or apply year-over-year comparison modes directly on the card.

3. **Explicit Widget Metadata:** HighLevel has added the date property and date range right onto the face of the widget. If a client asks why two numbers look different, the exact parameters are visible at a glance, eliminating confusion.

### Practical Implications for Agency Operations

If you manage client onboarding, this update cuts down setup time significantly. Previously, giving a client a segmented view meant building out custom reports or relying heavily on exported data and external visualization tools. Now, you can take a standard template, duplicate widgets, apply permanent filters for specific geographic regions or lead sources, and rename them with client-friendly terminology.

For example, if you run a multi-location franchise account, you can duplicate a standard pipeline widget four times, filter each one by the respective location's custom field, and give the local manager an instant, un-cluttered view of their branch's performance without letting them accidentally mess with global filters.

To see how these configuration updates fit into a broader client management strategy, take a look at the platform capabilities when you [explore HighLevel](https://www.gohighlevel.com/?fp_ref=affiliatealfa) and test out how it handles multi-user permissions and custom reporting.

### How to Set It Up

Head over to any sub-account dashboard and click **Edit dashboard** in the top right corner. Hover over the general widget you want to modify, click the three-dot menu, and select **Edit**. From there, you can apply your conditions, drop in a visual theme that matches your client's branding, and save. The filters persist every single time the dashboard is loaded.

This update moves HighLevel dashboards closer to true enterprise-grade reporting, giving agencies the granular control they need to keep clients focused on the metrics that actually matter.
