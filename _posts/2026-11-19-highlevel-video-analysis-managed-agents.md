If you run an agency, your team likely spends hours reviewing client webinars, parsing long-form video content for social media clips, or manually auditing ad creatives. It is tedious, necessary, and eats up valuable billable hours. HighLevel just rolled out a native solution that changes how we handle video assets inside Agent Studio: Video Analysis for Managed Agents.

Instead of forcing your team to scrub through a 45-minute client interview or a lengthy training webinar to find specific soundbites, your Managed Agents can now watch, listen, and extract insights from video files up to 2 GB or direct YouTube links. This goes beyond simple transcription. The engine evaluates both the visual cues and the audio track, meaning your AI can spot brand logos, check ad compliance, and pull timestamped highlights in a matter of seconds.

### Practical Agency Use Cases

How does this actually impact your daily operations? Consider the typical content repurposing workflow. A client hands you a monthly 30-minute industry webinar. Previously, a content manager had to watch the whole thing, take notes, and manually carve out clips. Now, you can point a Managed Agent to the file and prompt it to generate a detailed summary, pull out key quotes, and draft a month's worth of LinkedIn posts or Twitter threads based strictly on the discussion.

Another heavy lift for growing agencies is quality assurance. If you manage paid media for e-commerce or local service clients, reviewing video ads for compliance and visual standards is a bottleneck. You can feed draft advertisements directly to your agent and ask it to verify whether specific product messaging or brand guidelines are met before it ever hits the ad manager. 

### Behind the Technical Details

HighLevel has integrated this directly into Agent Studio without requiring external API keys or third-party wrappers. It handles common formats like MP4, MOV, and WebM. During internal benchmarks, processing a 24-minute recording took under a minute. Billing is handled through standard token usage, meaning you aren't paying a separate per-video fee every time you test a prompt or analyze a new file.

For agencies looking to scale their automation offerings, combining video parsing with existing CRM workflows opens up entirely new service tiers. Imagine an automated onboarding sequence where a client uploads their brand video, the agent reviews it for core messaging, extracts key FAQs for the knowledge base, and flags any missing brand elements automatically.

### Getting Started

You don't need to configure a complex web hook or toggle hidden settings to try this out. Navigate to your sub-account, open up **AI Agents > Agent Studio > Managed Agents**, select an existing agent or spin up a new one, and drop in a public YouTube link or a media library video. Prompt the agent for a transcript, timestamped highlights, or a social media brief. 

Features like this show why moving client operations into an all-in-one platform continues to pay off. Drop the manual video review process and let your agents handle the heavy lifting while your team focuses on strategy.
