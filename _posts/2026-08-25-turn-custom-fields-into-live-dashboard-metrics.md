If you run a marketing agency, you know how frustrating it is to track client KPIs across fragmented tools. Until now, pulling meaningful numeric and monetary custom fields—like deal values, treatment costs, or property sizes—into client-facing dashboards required manual data exports, spreadsheets, or third-party reporting connectors. HighLevel just solved that bottleneck with a straightforward update that changes how sub-account reporting works.

### What Changed in Sub-Account Dashboards

You can now use numeric and monetary custom fields on both Contacts and Opportunities directly as native metrics. Instead of restricting these custom data points to mere filters or table groupings, HighLevel lets you aggregate them instantly using standard mathematical functions: Sum, Min, Max, and Average.

This functionality isn't hidden away in a secondary reporting tab, either. It is fully integrated wherever metrics live across the platform, including standard dashboard widgets, custom reports, and the dedicated Custom Metric module.

Even better? Zero setup is required. The moment you create a new numeric or monetary custom field in a sub-account, it populates automatically in the metric picker, ready for immediate calculation. 

### Why This Matters for Client Reporting

Agency clients care about hard numbers tied to revenue, not just lead volume. If you are managing real estate agents, home service contractors, or high-ticket medical clinics, your pipelines track critical financial metrics that standard CRM fields simply do not capture.

Consider a real estate client where agents log custom property values on each deal. Previously, showing the total pipeline volume or average deal size required exporting contacts into CSV files and running formulas manually every Monday morning. Now, you can build a live dashboard widget that aggregates property values instantly using a simple Sum or Average calculation.

This completely eliminates manual math and keeps your clients inside the white-labeled sub-account environment rather than forcing them to decipher messy spreadsheets. It makes your agency look sharper and saves your account managers hours of recurring manual reporting time every month.

### Practical Implementation Tips

To get the most out of this update across your client sub-accounts, audit your current custom fields today:

1. **Standardize Field Types:** Ensure your financial and numeric data points are explicitly set as numeric or monetary fields rather than text inputs. Text fields cannot be aggregated.
2. **Clean Up Naming Conventions:** Give your custom fields clear, client-friendly labels (e.g., "Estimated Deal Value" instead of "Field_492") so they make sense when selected in dashboard widgets.
3. **Build Template Dashboards:** Update your agency's snapshot templates to include these custom metric widgets by default, meaning every new client account you spin up comes pre-configured with advanced revenue tracking.
