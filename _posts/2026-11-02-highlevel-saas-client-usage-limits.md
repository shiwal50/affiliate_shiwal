Running an agency SaaS model comes with a tricky balancing act. You want your clients to lean heavily into automation and AI tools because that proves value, but unmonitored consumption can quietly eat into your profit margins. Until now, managing runaway client usage meant either absorbing unexpected carrier and AI token bills or having awkward, reactive conversations after a feature temporarily stopped working.

HighLevel has introduced a practical fix currently rolling out in Labs for Pro agencies: hard usage limits for client sub-accounts, starting specifically with high-cost features like Voice AI and Conversation AI. 

### How Client Usage Limits Work in Practice

Instead of treating AI usage as an open-ended resource, agency owners can now cap what each client sub-account can consume within a billing cycle. When you onboard a new client with rebilling, setting these guardrails can become a standard part of your setup workflow.

Clients aren't left in the dark either. Each sub-account billing page displays a real-time meter showing current consumption against their allocated limit. Color-coded cues change as they approach their threshold, alongside clear reset dates so they always know where they stand.

If a client hits their cap and cannot upgrade their SaaS tier directly, they aren't hit with a dead end. Instead, they see a prompt to request a higher limit. That request routes directly to your agency dashboard and the SaaS Configurator. You get an immediate in-app and email notification, allowing you to review the request, adjust their limit, and bill them accordingly without ever leaving your management screen.

### Why This Changes the Agency Business Model

For agencies utilizing [HighLevel's SaaS mode](https://www.gohighlevel.com/protrial?fp_ref=affiliatealfa), this feature bridges the gap between flat-rate subscriptions and usage-based billing. Rather than eating the cost of heavy AI callers or overly chatty lead-qualification bots, you can package standard limits into your monthly tier and monetize every tier above that.

It shifts your relationship with clients from reactive firefighting to proactive upselling. When a local business client outgrows their baseline AI allotment, it signals that their campaigns are working. That gives you an organic opening to approve a higher limit and capture additional monthly recurring revenue to cover the underlying API costs.
