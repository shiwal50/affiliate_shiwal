HighLevel has updated the AI Agent Action inside workflows to include models from Anthropic and Google alongside OpenAI. If you are building automated marketing routines or client communication sequences, this change gives you much finer control over how tasks are handled, how much reasoning power is applied, and what your token costs look like.

### What’s New in the AI Agent Action

Previously, the workflow AI builder relied entirely on OpenAI configurations. The updated model picker now groups options by provider across three major ecosystems:

* **Anthropic:** Claude Opus 5, Claude Sonnet 5, and Claude Haiku 4.5.
* **Google:** Gemini 3.6 Flash and Gemini 3.1 Pro Preview.
* **OpenAI:** GPT-5.6 Luna, GPT-5.6 Tera, GPT-5.6 Sol, and GPT-5 Nano.

Beyond just adding more names to a dropdown, HighLevel has integrated a thinking chip for models that support reasoning. You can now adjust effort levels—choosing Low, Medium, or High directly from the action settings. Selecting a higher effort setting lets the model execute deeper multi-step reasoning for complex logic, while lower effort keeps things fast and efficient for straightforward checks.

### Why This Matters for Agency Operations

For marketing agencies managing client sub-accounts, using a single powerhouse model for every minor action is an unnecessary expense. With this update, you can match the model to the specific job:

1. **Intent Classification and Fast Reads:** Use a lighter, faster model like Gemini Flash or a lower effort setting to quickly categorize incoming lead replies or tag basic contact behaviors.
2. **Complex Multi-Step Logic:** Route intricate lead qualification, tailored copy generation, or conditional decision-making through flagship models like Claude or the higher-tier GPT options, applying High/Medium effort where precision is mandatory.

HighLevel reports that when combined with previously released token optimizations, these routing choices can run tasks up to 50% cheaper. That margin adds up quickly across dozens of client portals running heavy automation day in and day out.

### How to Implement It

Open any workflow, drop in or select an existing AI Agent Action, and click the model dropdown. You will see the new provider groupings along with built-in descriptions to help you judge which model fits your objective. Set your reasoning effort, test the trigger, and publish.
