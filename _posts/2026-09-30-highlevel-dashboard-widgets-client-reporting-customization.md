Agency client reporting often involves a delicate balance between showing enough data to prove value and avoiding confusion over metrics that do not apply to a specific stakeholder. For years, HighLevel sub-account dashboards presented a generic view where every user saw the exact same top-level numbers upon logging in. If a client wanted to focus solely on their individual performance, or if an agency wanted to present a clean, tailored view without background noise, manual filtering was required during every single session.

That friction is now gone with the latest update allowing full configuration and permanent filtering of general widgets in sub-account dashboards. Instead of accepting a one-size-fits-all reporting interface, agency operators can finally lock down data views per widget, transforming a messy generic overview into a clean, client-ready command center.

### Permanent Filtering and Custom Conditions

The standout addition here is the new Conditions tab inside the widget editor. Rather than relying on temporary session filters, you can now permanently scope any general widget using concrete logic. You can filter by assigned user, specific tags, lead source, custom fields, and attribution models.

Consider a multi-user sub-account where local sales reps or support agents log in to check their pipeline. By setting the assigned user condition to the currently logged-in user once, every team member automatically sees only their own metrics the moment they open the page. No more manual toggling, and no more accidental peeking at company-wide data that distracts from individual quotas.

### Fixing the Date Range Problem

Another historical pain point has been the rigid nature of dashboard-level date filters. Previously, changing the global date filter shifted every single card on the screen, sometimes breaking comparative views or forcing irrelevant time horizons onto specific KPIs.

With date range overrides, you can lock an individual widget to a specific historical window—such as Year-to-Date or Last Quarter—independent of what the global dashboard is currently displaying. Furthermore, the widget face now explicitly shows the date property and active range. When a client asks why two cards look different, the answer is printed directly on the UI, eliminating unnecessary support tickets for your account managers.

To make these reports truly shine for external stakeholders, agencies can rename widgets into plain, client-friendly language, apply custom themes that match brand guidelines, and utilize comparison modes to display current performance against the prior year or previous period.

### Practical Implementation for Agencies

To put this into practice today, navigate to your sub-account dashboard, click Edit dashboard in the top right corner, and open the three-dot menu on any general widget. From there, assign your permanent conditions, set your date overrides, and polish the presentation. By taking twenty minutes to configure these views per client, you elevate the perceived value of your retainer and ensure your reporting suite looks like it was custom-built from the ground up.
