If you manage client ad accounts directly inside HighLevel, you have likely run into the friction of rigid budget constraints. Until now, setting up Meta campaigns through the platform's Ad Manager meant dealing with limited flexibility when trying to balance spend across ad sets. HighLevel's latest update introduces full support for the Meta Campaign Budget Optimizer (CBO), giving media buyers the exact structural controls they need without having to jump back into native Ads Manager.

### What the New CBO Controls Change for Media Buyers

Previously, managing multi-ad set campaigns felt like a compromise. You either locked in a rigid ad set budget and manually shifted funds as performance fluctuated, or you struggled to align HighLevel workflows with Meta's optimization algorithms.

Now, you have two distinct paths right inside the HighLevel campaign builder:

1. **Campaign-Level Budgets (CBO):** Set a single daily or lifetime budget at the campaign level, and let Meta dynamically distribute that capital toward the ad sets driving the best results.
2. **Ad Set Budgets with 20% Sharing:** If you prefer granular control over individual ad sets, you can still define separate budgets for each one. Crucially, you can now toggle on a setting that permits Meta to share up to 20% of an ad set's budget with other eligible ad sets when performance spikes. 

Additionally, the update brings native support for advanced bid strategies like Highest Volume and Cost Per Result goals, along with post-publish budget adjustments. If a client wants to scale a winning campaign mid-flight, you can now modify these parameters directly from your dashboard.

### A Practical Agency Use Case

Imagine you are running a lead generation campaign for a local home services client across three distinct geographic ad sets. In the past, if Ad Set A ran out of qualified local leads while Ad Set B was crushing its cost-per-lead target, your budget was trapped. You had to manually reallocate funds, often hours after the opportunity had passed.

With the new budget-sharing toggle enabled, HighLevel allows Meta to automatically push up to 20% of underutilized funds from slower ad sets into high-performing ones in real time. Your client gets a lower blended cost per lead, and your media buyers spend less time babysitting minor budget allocations.
