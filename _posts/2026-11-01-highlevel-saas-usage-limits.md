If you are running a white-label agency on HighLevel, you have likely run into the dreaded runaway client. A local business client turns on Voice AI or Conversation AI, goes wild with automated calls, racks up thousands of minutes, and then treats the unexpected overage bill like a surprise tax. Until now, managing this meant either absorbing the costs yourself or waiting for the monthly invoice to trigger an uncomfortable dispute.

HighLevel has rolled out SaaS usage limits through Labs for Pro agencies, and it changes how you handle client consumption. You can now establish strict caps on usage-based features like Voice AI and Conversation AI during onboarding or directly from your agency dashboard. When a client approaches their threshold, they see clear color-coded warnings right on their billing page, eliminating the "I didn't know" excuse entirely.

For agency owners looking to scale, this update solves a major operational headache. Instead of hard-stopping a client's campaign—which can lead to churn when their lead flow abruptly dies—you can configure limits to prompt the client for more. When a client hits their cap, they get a direct "Request higher limit" button inside their billing portal. That request pops up immediately on your agency dashboard, letting you approve an expanded tier and charge accordingly with a single click.

### Why Usage Caps Matter for Agency Margins

When you package AI tools into your SaaS tiers, your margins live or die by predictability. A single heavy user can wipe out the profit of three quiet accounts if consumption goes unchecked. By utilizing these new controls, you shift the burden of monitoring off your own team's shoulders. The platform tracks utilization in real time, displays reset dates transparently to the end user, and turns an awkward overage conversation into a streamlined approval workflow.

If you want to protect your profitability while rolling out advanced automation to local businesses, setting up these guardrails is non-negotiable. It ensures that heavy usage directly translates into higher revenue rather than eaten margins. To see how these tools fit into a high-margin agency model, check out the [HighLevel SaaS Pro trial](https://www.gohighlevel.com/protrial?fp_ref=affiliatealfa) and test the controls inside Labs today.

### What to Watch For

Right now, usage limits target Voice AI and Conversation AI, though HighLevel has confirmed that email, SMS, and core phone systems are coming down the pipeline. Sub-account admins can view usage and request increases, but the power to set initial caps and approve bumps stays firmly in the hands of the agency owner and admins.
