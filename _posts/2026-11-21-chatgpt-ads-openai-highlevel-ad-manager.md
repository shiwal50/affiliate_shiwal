HighLevel has just added OpenAI as a native platform inside Ad Manager, letting marketing agencies build, target, and measure ads running directly inside ChatGPT. If your clients are trying to capture early-stage buyer research before prospects even hit traditional search engines, this update opens up a brand-new inventory source without forcing your media buying team to learn a separate platform.

### What the OpenAI Integration Entails

Instead of treating ChatGPT advertising as an external chore, HighLevel has integrated it right into the existing Ad Manager workflow. You can connect your OpenAI Ads account, configure your tracking pixels, set conversion events, and structure your campaigns using the familiar campaign, ad group, and ad hierarchy you already use for Meta, Google, and LinkedIn.

Ads appear natively across ChatGPT apps on iOS and Android, as well as on chatgpt.com on desktop and mobile. When setting up ad groups, you aren't relying on traditional keyword targeting. Instead, HighLevel utilizes context hints—descriptions of the exact situations your customers find themselves in, written in plain language. HighLevel even provides one-click suggested hints to get campaigns moving quickly.

### How Agencies Can Put This to Work

Consider how B2B software buyers or high-ticket service clients do research today. They are typing complex exploratory queries into ChatGPT long before they look at vendor pricing pages. By launching targeted campaigns via [HighLevel's AI tools](https://www.gohighlevel.com/ai?fp_ref=affiliatealfa), your agency can place a client's offering directly in front of active researchers at the exact moment of intent.

For example, if you manage a local commercial real estate firm, you can target context hints around businesses looking to relocate or expand office space. The ad creative itself remains straightforward: a 50-character title, a 100-character description, a destination landing page URL, and a square image. HighLevel provides a live preview as you type so you can verify how the ad looks before pushing it live.

### Audience Matching and Analytics

Retargeting and custom audiences are handled securely. You can upload an existing customer list or pull directly from a HighLevel smart list. Before any data reaches OpenAI, email addresses and phone numbers are hashed. Note that OpenAI requires a minimum of 25,000 matched users to target a custom audience, making this feature most effective for established client bases.

Once campaigns are live, performance data doesn't live in a silo. ChatGPT spend, clicks, and conversions feed straight back into HighLevel's Statistics tab, sitting right alongside your multi-channel reporting. With a standard 30-day click-through and 1-day view-through attribution window, proving ROI to clients stays straightforward.

If you want to stay ahead of where consumer search behavior is heading, testing ChatGPT ads through Ad Manager gives your agency an immediate early-mover advantage.
