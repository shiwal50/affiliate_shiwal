If you have ever pushed a complex automation live only to realize a missing custom field or an unmapped webhook broke the whole sequence, you know the frustration. HighLevel has finally rolled out a solution that stops broken logic before it reaches production: the Workflow Publish & Validation Gate.

### The Problem with Past Workflows

Previously, building automations meant playing a guessing game with validation. Frontend and backend errors lived in separate silos. You might clear one warning on the canvas, hit publish, and instantly get hit with a completely different error toaster from the backend. Worse, if your draft had lingering configuration issues, HighLevel often blocked you from saving altogether. You either had to hack together a temporary fix or scrap your progress mid-stream.

For agency owners managing dozens of client sub-accounts, these silent breaks create unnecessary support tickets. Clients notice when a lead drops into a pipeline without tags or fails to receive an automated SMS sequence.

### How the New Validation Gate Works

HighLevel has overhauled this entire experience by merging frontend and backend validation into a single, cohesive **Highlight Error sidebar**. When you click the publish button on a workflow with unresolved configuration issues, a modal now halts the process and lists every single error in one place.

Instead of hunting through nested triggers and actions, you get two direct paths to resolution:

1. **Open Step:** Jump straight to the broken action or trigger with a single click.
2. **AI Assistance:** Let HighLevel's built-in AI help you resolve the configuration error on the spot.

Crucially, **auto-save and manual save now work even when errors exist on the canvas**. You can build halfway through a complex logic branch, hit save to preserve your work, and walk away without fighting error pop-ups. The system lets you keep your progress while blocking you from taking the workflow live until every issue is cleared.

### Why This Matters for Agencies

As agency builders scale their automation offerings, infrastructure stability is everything. When you onboard new clients or deploy complex snapshots, the last thing you want is faulty logic leaking into live environments.

This update builds on a full year of underlying validation improvements across HighLevel's ecosystem. By ensuring that only clean, working workflows can hit the publish button, it drastically reduces debugging time and protects your client relationships from embarrassing automation failures.
