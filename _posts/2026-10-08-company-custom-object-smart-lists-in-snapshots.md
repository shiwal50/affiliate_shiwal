If your agency builds custom sub-accounts for clients, you already know the friction involved in setting up repetitive CRM architecture. For years, deploying a new sub-account meant manually recreating Smart Lists, configuring filters, and hoping your team didn't miss a nuanced tag or field condition when replicating a setup across different client verticals.

HighLevel has just removed a major bottleneck for multi-account management. You can now include Company and Custom Object Smart Lists directly inside Account Snapshots. 

### Why This Changes Client Onboarding

Smart Lists dictate how your team—and your clients—interact with their database on a daily basis. Whether you are segmenting B2B accounts by tier, tracking real estate properties by city and price bracket, or monitoring software subscriptions nearing expiration, these views form the backbone of everyday agency operations.

Previously, Snapshots handled standard contact filters easily, but pushing a sub-account live meant your operations team still had to spend hours manually rebuilding company-level and custom object segments. If you manage dozens of sub-accounts, that repetitive configuration work adds up to hundreds of wasted billable hours a year.

Now, you can configure these segments once in your master account and push them globally. When you deploy or refresh a Snapshot, those Company and Custom Object Smart Lists carry right over. ### Practical Implementation for Agency Builders

To make this feature work smoothly across your client base, follow a structured rollout process:

1. **Build and Test Locally:** Construct your Company or Custom Object Smart Lists in a staging sub-account first. Run a few test records through your filters to ensure the logic catches exactly what it should.
2. **Bundle the Dependencies:** Remember that a Smart List is only as good as the underlying data structure. When adding lists to your Snapshot, verify that all associated custom fields and objects are also included in the Snapshot package.
3. **Push and Verify:** Once the Snapshot is loaded into a destination sub-account, spot-check the lists to ensure the filter parameters translated accurately without orphaned data fields.

By standardizing these segments into your core agency build, you cut down manual configuration errors and ensure every new client starts with a fully optimized, turnkey CRM environment from day one.
