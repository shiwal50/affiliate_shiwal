Managing client reputation management at scale just got significantly easier for marketing agencies using HighLevel. Up until now, configuring Review AI agents meant diving into each sub-account individually. If you managed dozens or hundreds of local business clients, rolling out a standardized automated response strategy was a tedious, manual chore prone to missed accounts and inconsistent configurations.

HighLevel has finally solved this operational bottleneck by introducing bulk management controls for Reviews AI straight from the agency view. Instead of hopping between client dashboards, agency owners can now configure and standardize review automation across multiple locations in a matter of seconds.

### Where to Find the New Bulk Controls

To access the updated management suite, navigate to your Agency view and head over to **AI Suite > AI Setup**. From this centralized dashboard, you will see a comprehensive list of your sub-accounts along with their current Review AI statuses.

From here, you can select one, several, or all of your sub-accounts simultaneously and update their response modes in bulk. You have three primary settings to choose from:

* **Off:** Completely disables AI review processing for the selected sub-accounts.
* **Suggestive:** Generates AI responses for incoming reviews, but requires human approval before they get published publicly.
* **Auto:** Fully automates the process, letting the AI generate and publish responses instantly based on your guardrails and brand voice settings.

If a sub-account administrator makes a local adjustment under their own settings, the agency view stays automatically synchronized, preventing any conflicting operational states.

### Practical Implications for Agency Operations

For agencies running local SEO or reputation management retainers, this update eliminates a major scaling friction point. When you onboard a new batch of locations or want to upgrade your existing client base from suggestive review replies to fully automated publishing, you no longer need a virtual assistant spending an entire afternoon clicking through individual dashboards.

Furthermore, if you need to quickly shut down automated responses across the board due to an edge-case issue or compliance shift, you can execute a global override instantly. It gives agency operators top-down governance while preserving local autonomy where needed.

### Safeguarding Your Clients

While scaling automation is the ultimate goal for agency growth, safety remains paramount. Disabling Reviews AI is also fully supported at the granular level through **Agency > Sub-Accounts > Features & Limits**. When you toggle it off there, it strips away the AI reply and regenerate buttons entirely, blocking manual AI drafting, auto-responses, and Drip Campaign replies at the root level.
