Managing B2B pipelines inside a CRM usually means dealing with a messy influx of contacts that float around unattached to any organization. Agency operators know the tedious reality: every time a new lead fills out a form, someone on the team has to manually check if the company exists, create a profile, attach the logo, and link the contact. It slows down sales velocity and introduces duplicate records that ruin smart lists and reporting.

HighLevel has just rolled out a native solution to this exact bottleneck. The new Automatic Company Creation and Association feature uses email domains to handle organizational housekeeping completely in the background. When a new contact enters your system, the CRM evaluates their email domain, scans for an existing company record, and associates them automatically. If no match is found, it spins up a new company profile, populates the unique domain name field, and even auto-enchriches the record with the company name and logo.

### Why This Changes B2B Agency Workflows

For agencies running outbound lead generation or inbound campaigns for B2B clients, data hygiene is everything. Previously, relying on business names for matching often led to fragmentation—one lead listed as "Acme Corp," another as "Acme Corporation," and a third as "Acme, Inc." Result? Three separate company records for the same account.

By anchoring association to exact domain matches, this update stops duplicates at the door. Common free email domains like Gmail and Yahoo are automatically filtered out, ensuring your CRM only groups true business leads. Furthermore, HighLevel smart enough to look at existing company records that might be missing a domain; when a matching email arrives, it backfills the domain data into the legacy record rather than duplicating it.

### Implementation Strategy for Agency Accounts

To put this into practice across your sub-accounts, you will want to approach the rollout methodically:

1. Head into **Settings > Labs** inside your sub-account and enable the feature.
2. Navigate to **Settings > Objects > Companies** to configure your automation parameters.
3. Turn on company enrichment and automatic prevention of duplicate entries.
4. Set up your exclusion list to filter out any specific partner or internal domains you want the system to ignore.

Once active, this background automation frees your sales reps and account managers from manual data entry. It ensures that reporting dashboards reflect accurate company-level engagement rather than isolated individual contacts.
