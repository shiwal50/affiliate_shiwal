HighLevel has just added OpenAI as a native platform inside Ad Manager, placing it right alongside Meta, Google, and LinkedIn. For marketing agencies, this update means you can now build, target, and measure ads running directly inside ChatGPT without needing to juggle external ad dashboards or leave your sub-account.

### The Shift in User Research Behavior

Users increasingly bypass traditional search engines to perform initial product and service research inside conversational AI tools like ChatGPT. By enabling native ad placements on chatgpt.com and within the mobile apps on iOS and Android, agencies can secure client visibility during the earliest research phase of the buyer journey.

The setup process leverages the same structural familiarity your media buyers already know: campaign, ad group, and ad hierarchies mirror what is currently used for Google or Meta. You can select objectives for reach, clicks, or conversions, set daily or lifetime budgets, and apply specific geographic targeting parameters.

### Targeting with Context Hints Instead of Keywords

Instead of traditional keyword targeting, OpenAI ad groups rely on context hints. Media buyers describe the specific user situations and intent states where an ad belongs in everyday language, and HighLevel even provides one-click suggested hints to speed up ad group creation. 

Ad creative is streamlined for conversational surfaces: a 50-character title, a 100-character description, a destination landing page URL, and a single square image. A live preview updates in real time within the dashboard as you type your copy. 

To maximize relevance, agencies can build custom audiences using customer lists or existing HighLevel smart lists. Contact data is securely hashed before transmission, ensuring personal identifiers never leave your control, though campaigns require a minimum match of 25,000 users to serve ads effectively.

### Unified Reporting and Analytics

Cross-channel reporting has historically been a bottleneck for multi-platform agencies. With this update, ChatGPT ad spend, clicks, and conversions integrate directly into HighLevel Statistics alongside your existing Meta and Google data. You can filter performance by device, location, and platform segments within a single interface.

For agencies looking to expand their service offerings into conversational search platforms, this integration removes technical friction and keeps campaign management under one roof.
