If your agency manages a high volume of inbound leads, keeping your sales and support teams aligned is probably one of your biggest daily bottlenecks. Until now, dropping context, leaving instructions, or tagging specific staff members inside a conversation thread required someone on your team to manually type out an internal note. If an agent got busy, that vital context stayed in someone's head instead of living where it belongs—directly inside the client conversation history.

HighLevel just rolled out a native workflow action that changes this entirely: you can now automatically add internal comments to conversations using standard automations. 

### How Automated Internal Comments Work

When building out your automations under Automation > Workflows, you now have access to a dedicated "Add Internal Comment" action. Instead of just firing off an SMS to a lead or updating a pipeline stage, the workflow can post a customized note straight into the conversation feed. 

Even better, you can @mention specific team members right inside the comment text. 

Imagine a high-value lead fills out a custom intake form on your client's website. They hit your pipeline, and a workflow immediately fires. Alongside your automated lead-assignment actions, the system can now drop an internal comment into the thread reading: 

> *"New VIP lead from the Q3 webinar funnel. @Sarah, please review their budget requirements and call within 15 minutes."*

Because these notes live inside the conversation history, your team gets instant context the second they open the thread. There is no need to jump over to Slack, dig through email chains, or check a separate project management tool to figure out what happened upstream. The customer context stays tethered directly to the communication record, completely invisible to the end client.

### Setting It Up in Minutes

Getting started takes just a few clicks:

1. Head over to **Automation > Workflows** in your sub-account.
2. Open an existing automation or start a fresh sequence based on your preferred trigger (like a form submission, tag added, or incoming message).
3. Drop the **Internal Comment** action into your step tree.
4. Type out your desired note, using the @ symbol to pull in specific agency users.
5. Save and publish.

This update may seem small on paper, but for teams trying to scale client delivery without drowning in internal admin, it removes a massive manual speed bump. Set up your first automated comment today and keep your team entirely on the same page.
