If you run an agency on HighLevel, setting up a new sub-account for a client usually involves a fair bit of manual configuration. Rebuilding filters, recreating segments, and aligning data views for every single onboarding eats up hours of billable time. Fortunately, HighLevel has just rolled out an update that significantly streamlines this process.

You can now include Company and Custom Object Smart Lists directly inside Account Snapshots. Instead of manually rebuilding your core CRM segments from scratch every time you spin up a new client environment, you can configure your filters once at the agency level and deploy them instantly across multiple sub-accounts.

### The Operational Bottleneck of Client Onboarding

Think about how your agency segments data. Whether you are tracking B2B companies by industry tier, monitoring local service properties by price range, or flagging customer memberships nearing their expiration date, Smart Lists are the engine of your CRM workflows. They dictate who gets tagged, who enters a pipeline, and which leads trigger automated email sequences.

Previously, snapshots handled contacts and standard pipelines wonderfully, but complex architectures relying on custom objects and company-level segmentation required manual reconstruction in every destination account. Miss a filter condition or misspell a custom field parameter, and your downstream automations break on day one. It introduced human error into a process that desperately needs standardization.

### How to Deploy Segmented Snapshots

Implementing this update takes just a few steps within your agency dashboard:

1. Head over to **Agency View** and open **Account Snapshots**.
2. Create a new snapshot or select an existing one you use for onboarding.
3. Add the specific Company Smart Lists and Custom Object Smart Lists you want to bundle.
4. Ensure the underlying custom objects and fields tied to those lists are also included in the snapshot package.
5. Push or load the snapshot into your target sub-account.

By taking a few minutes to standardize your master setup, you ensure that every new client onboarding inherits clean, operational segments immediately upon provisioning.
