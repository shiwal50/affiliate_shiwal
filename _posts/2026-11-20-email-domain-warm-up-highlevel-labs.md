If you manage email campaigns for local clients, you already know the pain of spinning up a new sending domain. Until now, protecting sender reputation meant paying for third-party warm-up tools, juggling complex SMTP integrations, or manually choreographing sending limits to keep your cold outreach out of the spam folder. HighLevel has officially moved its native Email Domain Warm-Up out of beta and into Labs, making it available to every agency and sub-account with zero approval hoops to jump through.

### How Native Domain Warm-Up Works

Instead of forcing you to rely on external software, LC Email now handles the heavy lifting directly inside your sub-account. When you enable the feature, the platform automatically provisions dedicated warm-up mailboxes tied to your domain. These mailboxes send natural, threaded conversations back and forth, complete with automated replies, messages marked as important, and pulls out of spam folders.

This behavior trains major inbox providers like Google, Outlook, and Yahoo to recognize your domain as a legitimate, engaged sender. Best of all, your actual contact lists never see these warm-up emails. The platform isolates the warm-up traffic completely, meaning your client databases remain untouched while your infrastructure builds trust behind the scenes.

### Setting Up Your First Domain

Getting started takes just a few clicks, provided your dedicated domain is fully verified and has an active SSL certificate. Head over to Settings, open Labs, and toggle on Email Domain Warm-Up. From there, navigate to your Email Services settings, select your Dedicated Domain and IP card, and hit start.

You will be prompted to set a daily sending goal—such as 10,000 or 25,000 emails per day. The system takes that number as a ceiling, ramping up your volume gradually from zero. Your domain card updates with a health score from 0 to 100, letting you monitor deliverability metrics, DMARC/DKIM/SPF statuses, and inbox placement charts in real-time.

### What It Costs

For agencies looking to standardize client tech stacks, the pricing model is straightforward. HighLevel is keeping Domain Warm-Up entirely free through December 2, 2026. Starting December 3, 2026, active warm-up will cost a fractional $0.59 per 1,000 emails sent, billed alongside your standard LC Email sending charges. Because the system ramps volume slowly, early days cost pennies, and you can pause or completely halt the warm-up process at any moment if a client campaign shifts.

### Why This Matters for Agency Operations

Consolidating your client infrastructure inside a [single CRM platform](https://www.gohighlevel.com/?fp_ref=affiliatealfa) eliminates yet another monthly SaaS subscription and cuts down on onboarding friction. Managing deliverability natively means your team can provision a brand-new client domain, fire up automated warm-ups, and transition into live outbound campaigns without jumping across multiple tabs or external dashboards.

If you have been holding off on dedicated domain strategies due to the complexity of third-party warm-up apps, enabling this inside Labs is the lowest-friction way to bulletproof your client email infrastructure.
