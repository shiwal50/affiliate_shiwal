If you have been building custom web apps inside HighLevel using AI Studio, you have likely run into a frustrating gap: connecting a proper support widget usually meant bolting on a third-party script that lived outside your CRM ecosystem. Conversations got fragmented, leads fell through the cracks, and styling a generic chat bubble to match your custom application design took unnecessary frontend tinkering. 

HighLevel has closed that gap with the rollout of native live chat and email chat widgets built directly into AI Studio apps. Instead of embedding external code, you can now prompt AI Studio to add a real, functioning chat widget right into your project. Because it runs on HighLevel's core messaging infrastructure, every visitor interaction feeds straight into your main sub-account conversations without requiring messy API workarounds.

### How It Works Under the Hood

When you ask AI Studio to add a chat, it sets up a native widget in your account, names it after your project, and wires it cleanly into your application's layout. You can configure whether it acts as a live chat or an offline email catcher for after-hours traffic. Because the widget is rendered inside your app's code, it automatically inherits your site's fonts, colors, and design parameters. 

For agency operators, the real power lies in what happens after the chat deploys. Every conversation adheres to your established business hours, out-of-hours auto-responses, and spam protection rules. Built-in rate limits and automatic bot captchas keep junk traffic out, while secure visitor tokens ensure session data never leaks into your source code. 

Returning visitors pick up right where they left off, loading older message history as they scroll. If a visitor shares an attachment, it handles supported file formats smoothly. When the conversation concludes, chat ratings and satisfaction metrics flow back into your centralized reporting.

### Tracking Leads and Managing Settings

Tracking the ROI of a custom app widget used to require custom event listeners. Now, you can navigate to `More > Analytics > Chat Widgets` inside your project to review total chat loads, open rates, and visitor satisfaction scores. A unified lead table lists out contact details, chat types, and the specific landing page where the conversation started.

Adjusting widget behavior is just as straightforward. You can manage messaging texts, contact capture forms, and privacy consent notices directly inside AI Studio without navigating away to global sub-account settings.
