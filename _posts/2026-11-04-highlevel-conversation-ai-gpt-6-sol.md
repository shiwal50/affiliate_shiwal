HighLevel has rolled out a major under-the-hood upgrade for its Conversation AI feature, making GPT-6 Sol the default and recommended model for all new prompt-based agents. If you manage client accounts that rely heavily on automated chat handling, this update is worth immediate attention because it addresses the eternal trade-off in conversational AI: balancing reply accuracy against generation speed and API token costs.

Previously, agencies utilizing GPT-4.1 for client chatbots faced solid comprehension rates, but OpenAI's deprecation timeline meant a forced migration was inevitable. HighLevel's internal evaluation benchmark shows that GPT-6 Sol achieves a 98.4% pass rate, marking a notable jump from the 96.3% score posted by GPT-4.1. More importantly for agency margins, this accuracy bump comes at roughly the same cost per reply. For a deeper look at how these automation layers operate across the platform, you can check out [HighLevel's AI tools](https://www.gohighlevel.com/ai?fp_ref=affiliatealfa) to see what is possible for your client campaigns.

Speed is another critical factor when human-like chat is deployed for real-time lead capture. The new infrastructure logs a typical generation time of just 1.68 seconds, with the slowest 5% of responses clearing within 2.19 seconds. For older agents that were running on the deprecated GPT-5 or GPT-5 Mini models, HighLevel has automatically migrated them to GPT-6 Sol, resulting in reply generation speeds up to 4.5 times faster with zero manual intervention required from your team.

### Practical Implications for Agency Sub-Accounts

While new prompt-based agents automatically spin up on GPT-6 Sol, existing agents retain whatever model you previously selected. However, because OpenAI is retiring GPT-4.1, you should proactively audit your sub-accounts and manually switch any legacy agents over. To do this, head into AI Agents, open Conversation AI, navigate to the Build tab, select GPT-6 Sol from the Model menu, and save your changes.

Crucially, do not skip testing. Even though the underlying model is smarter and faster, every language model structures sentences differently. Always use the built-in testing utility to simulate typical prospect questions and verify that your system prompts still trigger the exact responses and booking links you expect.

For agencies scaling high-volume lead generation, faster and more accurate automated chat directly impacts conversion rates. Fewer dropped chats and more precise qualification mean better ROI on ad spend for your local business clients. Take a moment today to review your chatbot configurations across client sub-accounts and ensure everything is running on the latest architecture before older endpoints drop offline permanently.
