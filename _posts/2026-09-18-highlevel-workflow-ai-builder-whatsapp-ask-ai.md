If you build automations for clients all day, you know the routine: open the workflow builder, add a trigger, map out the branches, set up the actions, and test every single step. It is powerful, but it takes clicks. HighLevel just removed that friction by bringing the Workflow AI builder directly inside Ask AI, meaning you can now draft, structure, and edit complex multi-step automations entirely through a chat interface—even straight from WhatsApp.

### How Chat-Based Automation Changes Agency Workflows

Until now, building inside HighLevel meant keeping your hands on the desktop builder. With this update, Ask AI can invoke the underlying workflow agent from anywhere. If you are on your phone, out of the office, or simply deep in a strategy chat, you can type out what you want a sequence to do, and the AI drafts the complete logic on the spot.

For instance, you can message Ask AI: *"Build a lead nurture sequence that triggers when a form is submitted, waits two hours, sends an SMS, and creates a task if there is no reply within a day."* Instead of making you piece it together manually, the agent generates the workflow, renders a live preview right inside your chat panel, and lets you inspect triggers or actions instantly.

This makes rapid prototyping frictionless. If you want to change a wait timer or swap out an email template, you do not need to hunt through menus. Just tell the assistant what to adjust, and it updates the workflow architecture in place. When the logic looks solid, a single click drops you directly into the main workflow builder where you can run a final test and hit publish. 

### Why This Matters for Agency Efficiency

Agency owners and operations managers rarely build workflows while sitting peacefully at a desk with zero distractions. Often, automation ideas pop up during client calls, on the road, or while reviewing campaign performance on a mobile device. 

By pushing workflow creation into conversational channels like WhatsApp, HighLevel is cutting down the time it takes to move from a conceptual idea to a working draft. You get the speed of generative AI paired with the rigorous safety of the native builder, ensuring your production systems never get deployed without a proper test run. ### Practical Implementation Tips

* **Use Chat for Drafting, Not Publishing:** Let Ask AI handle the heavy lifting of mapping out complex branching logic and multi-channel sequences. Always use the final redirect link to open the standard builder for final testing and activation.
* **Leverage WhatsApp on the Go:** Keep the Ask AI contact pinned in your WhatsApp for those moments when a client asks for a quick automation tweak while you are away from your laptop.
* **Iterate Concurrently:** If a generated sequence needs minor tweaks, use conversational prompts to refine the steps iteratively before opening the desktop editor to review the final configuration.
