If you manage client reporting in HighLevel, you have likely run into an awkward reporting quirk. When building custom dashboards for local businesses or marketing clients, showing an upward trend in metrics like unsubscribe rates, bounce rates, or cost-per-lead always triggered a bright green badge. For non-marketers looking at a report, a green arrow heavily implies that something good just happened—even if their email list is burning through subscribers or their ad spend is spiraling out of control.

HighLevel has finally solved this issue with a simple yet essential update: the ability to configure whether an increase or a decrease on a widget's trend badge is treated as positive (green) or negative (red). 

### Why This Matters for Client Retention

Agency life relies heavily on perception and clarity. When you hand over a reporting dashboard or jump on a monthly review call, the last thing you want to spend time doing is explaining why a green arrow next to an email bounce rate actually means bad news. Clients scan numbers fast. They look for green as a win and red as a warning. 

By letting you flip the logic on metrics where lower is objectively better, HighLevel helps agencies build genuine trust. If a client's cost-per-lead drops week-over-week, that downward movement now rightfully shows up as a green positive indicator. If churn rates spike, it correctly triggers a red warning flag.

### How to Set It Up

Applying this fix takes just a few seconds per widget:

1. Open up your dashboard or custom report and head into the widget's **Configure** panel.
2. Under Advanced settings, ensure your **Date range override** and **Comparison date range** are active so the trend badge populates.
3. Locate the new setting governing how the metric should be read, and choose **Decrease is positive** instead of the default increase setting.
4. Save your changes.

The update applies cleanly across whatever chart format you choose, whether you are utilizing simple Number blocks, Line graphs, or Bar charts. Existing widgets will maintain their current baseline behavior ("Increase is positive") so you don't have to worry about old client reports suddenly shifting formats unexpectedly.
