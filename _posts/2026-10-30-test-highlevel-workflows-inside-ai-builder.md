If you build automations inside HighLevel using the chat-based AI Builder, you already know how fast it is to spin up a new sequence. You describe what you need, and the system drafts the trigger, the branching, and the actions on your canvas. But until now, the workflow development loop hit a frustrating speed bump right after generation.

You had to close out of the AI prompt, navigate away from your chat context, find a test contact, enroll them manually, and then dig through dense Execution Logs to figure out if every step actually fired correctly. If something broke—a missing custom field, a misconfigured tag, an email that refused to send—you had to jump back into the builder, tweak the prompt or the steps, and repeat the whole audit cycle.

HighLevel has finally removed that friction. You can now test your workflow right inside the AI Builder chat window the moment it finishes generating your automation.

### How the In-Chat Testing Loop Works

When the AI Builder finishes constructing or editing your workflow, its response now concludes with a direct **Test workflow** button. Clicking it opens the test interface right inside your chat panel.

You have two choices for your data source: enroll an existing contact or test using live system events. Before you hit start, you can verify the contact's data to ensure your test variables are clean. Once you initiate the run, the canvas visually updates in real time. Each step reports its status—marking items as running, success, action skipped, or end—so you can visually audit the path.

At the same time, the chat interface outputs a concise plain-English summary of the execution run. It tells you explicitly which steps completed, which ones skipped, and where any errors or missing data tripped up the logic. If you need to inspect inputs and outputs for a specific action, you can just click that step on the canvas without leaving your view.

### Practical Implications for Agency Builders

For agency owners and automation specialists, this update cuts debugging time down to seconds. When you are deploying complex client onboarding sequences or multi-tier lead nurturing pipelines, verifying logic quickly is essential.

Because the test executes real steps—meaning tags are applied, notes are written, and messages go out—you should always use an internal testing contact or a dummy profile when running these checks. It acts as a live fire test rather than a sandbox simulation, giving you absolute confidence before you flip the switch to production.

This continuous workflow iteration speeds up delivery times for client builds.
