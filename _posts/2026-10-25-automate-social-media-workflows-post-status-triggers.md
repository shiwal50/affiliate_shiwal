Managing social media across multiple client brands usually involves a mountain of manual tracking. You schedule posts, cross your fingers that they publish correctly, and chase down team members for approvals. If a post fails, you often only find out when a client points it out. HighLevel has just rolled out a native update that changes this dynamic entirely by bringing post triggers directly into Workflows.

### What Just Shipped

You can now use Social Planner post events as triggers inside your automation builder. Instead of treating social scheduling as a siloed task, your published content, scheduled queues, and failed posts can kick off automated internal actions or client updates. 

The triggers cover the full lifecycle of a social post:

* **Approval Management:** Automatically move things forward when a post is submitted, approved, or rejected.
* **Publishing Status:** Build logic around scheduled posts or successfully published items.
* **Failure Alerts:** Instantly notify your team if a post hits a snag so someone can fix it before the client notices.

On top of tracking statuses, you can filter these workflows by content category, specific platforms like LinkedIn or TikTok, post types such as Reels or Carousels, and even individual social accounts or tags. 

### A Practical Agency Use Case

Imagine you run social media for a dozen local service businesses. In the past, handling failed posts meant someone had to manually check the Social Planner dashboard every morning. Now, you can build a single master workflow for failure alerts. 

If a post fails on Instagram or Facebook, the workflow instantly pings the assigned account manager via internal SMS, creates a high-priority task in the CRM, and logs the incident. Your team fixes the token error or media format within minutes rather than days. 

Similarly, you can automate client approval loops. When a junior copywriter submits a batch of promotional posts, the workflow can instantly ping the account manager with a review link, removing the need for clunky external spreadsheets or email chains.

### Why This Matters for Scaling Agencies

Most CRM and marketing platforms treat social publishing as a dead-end endpoint. You push content out, and that is where the integration stops. By tying social activity back into the core automation engine, HighLevel lets you treat social media as an active part of your broader client operations.
