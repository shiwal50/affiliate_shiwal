If you are running complex multi-step automations inside HighLevel workflows, relying solely on OpenAI for your AI agent actions is officially a thing of the past. HighLevel just rolled out native support for Anthropic and Google models inside the workflow AI agent action, giving agency builders a massive expansion of horsepower right where it counts.

### What Changed in the AI Agent Action

Previously, your workflow AI steps were tethered entirely to OpenAI. Now, the updated model picker groups your options neatly by provider. You can pick from Anthropic's lineup (including Claude Opus 5, Sonnet 5, and Haiku 4.5) or tap into Google's ecosystem (featuring Gemini 3.6 Flash and 3.1 Pro Preview), alongside the familiar OpenAI options.

Even better, HighLevel added a couple of critical interface upgrades that make managing these models much easier:

* **Thinking Chips:** Models that support advanced reasoning now feature a clear visual indicator so you know exactly which ones can handle heavy logical lifting.
* **Effort Control:** A new dropdown lets you toggle reasoning effort between Low, Medium, and High. 

### Why This Matters for Agency Automations

For agency owners building client-facing or internal automations, this update solves two major hurdles: cost efficiency and task matching.

Not every workflow action requires a flagship model. If you are running intent classification, parsing a simple incoming email, or doing basic string reads, routing that task through an expensive, heavy model is a waste of capital. With these new choices, you can pair a fast, lightweight model for routine data checks, while reserving deep reasoning models for complex, multi-step tasks where accuracy is non-negotiable.

Furthermore, the new effort control slider adds a second layer of optimization. By dialing down the reasoning effort on simpler tasks, you get faster response times and lower token consumption. Layered on top of recent token optimizations, HighLevel notes that routine tasks can run up to 50% cheaper.

### Putting It Into Practice

Imagine an inbound lead qualification workflow. Instead of using a one-size-fits-all approach, you can route initial message parsing through a fast Google or Anthropic model to extract intent instantly. If the lead triggers high-value conditional logic requiring nuanced evaluation, hand the context over to a high-effort reasoning model before notifying your sales team or booking an appointment.
