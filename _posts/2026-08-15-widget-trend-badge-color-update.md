If you manage client reporting dashboards in HighLevel, you have probably had to explain a frustrating quirk: a metric goes down, which is great for the client, but HighLevel slaps a glaring red warning arrow next to it.

Take cost-per-lead, email bounce rates, unsubscribe figures, or churn. When those numbers drop, your agency is doing its job. But previously, the platform's trend badges assumed every single metric operated on a "higher is always better" logic. Handing a report to a local business owner with a sea of red arrows next to declining acquisition costs takes a lot of unnecessary explaining. It shakes client confidence instantly, forcing you to waste review call time clarifying that red actually means success in specific contexts.

HighLevel has finally fixed this reporting trap. You can now configure trend badge colors per widget to match reality. When building or editing custom reports and dashboards, you can open a widget's advanced settings and flip the toggle from "Increase is positive" to "Decrease is positive."

### Practical Agency Implementation

To get the most out of this update, take 15 minutes to audit your standard agency dashboard templates. Any metric tracking negative events needs an immediate adjustment:

* **Unsubscribe and Bounce Rates:** A rising rate is bad, so a falling rate should now proudly display a green positive badge.
* **Cost Per Acquisition (CPA) or Cost Per Lead (CPL):** As you optimize ad spend, these numbers should shrink. Setting these to reward a decrease makes your efficiency gains instantly obvious at a glance.
* **Support Ticket Volume or Client Churn:** If you are tracking operational metrics alongside marketing performance, downward trends here signal better client health.

This setting applies universally across whichever chart type you choose—whether you are using simple number stats, line graphs, or bar charts. Once you save the configuration in the widget's settings panel, the comparison date range logic handles the rest automatically.

### Building Real Client Trust

Client reporting is about reducing friction and proving value without needing a translated lecture. When local business owners log into their reporting portals, they look for instant visual cues. Green means good, red means bad. By aligning your dashboard indicators with actual business outcomes, you eliminate ambiguity and reinforce your agency's impact.
