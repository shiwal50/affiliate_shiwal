If you manage client reporting inside HighLevel, you have likely run into the universal dashboard dilemma. You build a clean sub-account dashboard, hand the login over to a client or a sales rep, and immediately realize they are staring at metrics they should not see. Up until now, general dashboard widgets pulled wide-open data for whoever logged in, forcing agencies to build out messy workarounds or separate reporting views just to keep things segmented.

That friction just disappeared. HighLevel rolled out a major update allowing you to configure, filter, and lock general widgets directly on sub-account dashboards. 

### What You Can Now Control

The update introduces a dedicated configuration layer right inside the dashboard editor. When you click into any general widget, you now have tabs for configuration, conditions, and themes. Here is why this changes how you structure client portals:

* **Permanent Conditions:** You can lock a widget down by assigned user, tags, source, lead value, custom fields, and attribution. If you set a widget to show only opportunities where the assigned user is the logged-in user, your sales reps automatically see only their own pipeline metrics the second they open the page.
* **Date Range Overrides:** You no longer have to rely solely on the global dashboard-level date filter. You can lock a specific widget to a permanent timeframe—like year-over-year growth—so a client sees their trajectory instantly without changing dropdowns.
* **Clearer Data Faces:** The widget itself now explicitly displays the date property and range it is calculating. That eliminates the classic confusion of two similar widgets showing different numbers because they were tied to conflicting parameters.
* **Comparison Modes:** You can toggle comparative metrics directly onto the face, contrasting the current period against the previous period or last year.
* **Client-Ready Branding:** You can rename widgets to plain, client-friendly language, apply a visual theme that matches their brand palette, and duplicate components to build segmented views.

### A Practical Agency Use Case

Imagine running a multi-location franchise client or a local agency team with distinct sales reps. Previously, your primary dashboard showed aggregate numbers, requiring sales reps to dig through filters manually every single morning. 

Now, you can duplicate a standard leaderboard widget three times, set permanent user-assignment filters on each, and drop them into a shared view. Alternatively, you can set a permanent lead-source filter to isolate paid ad traffic from organic inbound leads on two separate graphs. It turns a generic reporting shell into something that genuinely looks and feels custom-built for that specific client.

### How to Set It Up

Head over to any sub-account dashboard, hit **Edit dashboard** in the top right corner, and hover over the three-dot menu on any general widget. From there, you can apply your permanent filters under the Conditions tab, lock down your timeframes in Advanced Settings, and apply a cohesive visual theme.

While pipeline stages still function as runtime filters rather than permanent conditions, this update handles the heavy lifting for general metrics. It saves hours of manual client hand-holding and makes your sub-account setups look significantly more professional out of the box.
