If you manage marketing reporting for local business clients, you have probably run into an awkward conversation caused by misleading dashboard metrics. Imagine showing a client their monthly report where their unsubscribe rate spiked, but the widget displayed a bright, cheerful green arrow next to it simply because the raw number moved upward. 

For agency owners, these minor UI quirks create unnecessary friction. Clients naturally associate green with positive performance and red with trouble. When metrics where lower is better—such as churn rate, cost per lead, bounce rates, or unsubscribe percentages—showed a green trend badge simply because volume increased, it forced marketers to constantly add verbal caveats during review calls.

HighLevel has finally solved this issue with a simple yet much-needed reporting update. You can now configure widget trend badge colors in dashboards and custom reports to decide whether an increase or a decrease should be classified as positive or negative.

### How the New Trend Badge Controls Work

When building or editing any number, line, or bar chart widget that utilizes a comparison date range, you are no longer locked into the default assumption that higher numbers are always better. 

Inside the widget's configure panel under advanced settings, a new toggle lets you switch the logic from "Increase is positive" to "Decrease is positive." 

Once activated, metrics like email bounce rates or ad acquisition costs will correctly display a green badge when they trend downward, and a red badge when they creep up. Existing widgets default safely to the original behavior, meaning none of your current client dashboards will unexpectedly change overnight unless you manually adjust them.

### Why This Matters for Client Retention

Transparency and clarity are the cornerstones of a strong agency-client relationship. When business owners log into their reporting portals or review automated emails sent from your [marketing agency platform](https://www.gohighlevel.com/?fp_ref=affiliatealfa), they expect the visual cues to tell the truth at a glance.

By ensuring that green always means genuine growth and red always highlights an area requiring attention, you eliminate ambiguity. It builds immediate trust and positions your agency as detail-oriented and professional. You spend less time explaining why a green arrow actually means a bad thing, and more time discussing strategy and optimizations.

### Putting It Into Practice

Take twenty minutes this week to audit your primary client-facing dashboards. Identify every custom widget tracking cost, attrition, or friction metrics—such as lead cost or campaign drop-off rates. Flip the configuration setting to ensure decreases are properly marked as positive.

It is a small configuration change, but it completely removes one more point of friction in client communications, making your reporting suite look cleaner, sharper, and far more accurate.
