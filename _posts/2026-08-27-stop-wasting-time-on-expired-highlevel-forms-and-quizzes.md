If you run time-sensitive marketing campaigns for clients—think flash sales, webinar registrations, or local coupon drops—you already know the headache of manual cleanup. Historically, once you published a HighLevel form, survey, or quiz, it stayed live indefinitely. Miss the exact moment the promotion ends, and leads keep pouring into an expired offer, forcing you to manually untangle support requests and issue refunds or rain checks.

HighLevel has finally solved this operational drag by rolling out native schedule visibility for forms, surveys, and quizzes. Instead of relying on calendar reminders or scrambling to unpublish assets at midnight, you can now automate the entire lifecycle of a lead capture page.

### How Automated Scheduling Works

When editing any form, survey, or quiz, you will now find a new scheduling toggle in the settings menu. You can define exact opening and closing dates tied to a specific time zone, completely removing human error from campaign launches and deadlines.

Even better, the platform lets you dictate what happens on either side of that window:

* **Pre-Launch Mode:** Build pages ahead of time and display a custom "coming soon" message or countdown before the official start time.
* **Post-Expiry Customization:** Once the deadline passes, automatically swap the active form for a custom notice or seamlessly redirect traffic to a brand-new URL.

For agencies managing multiple clients, this is a massive operational win. Local businesses running weekend coupon campaigns can finally stop calling you on Sunday night to take down an expired lead form. You set the parameters once during setup, and the system handles the transition automatically.

### Practical Agency Applications

Beyond basic event registrations, this feature opens up cleaner funnels for standard client use cases:

1. **Evergreen and Fixed-Window Webinars:** Stop traffic the moment a training session starts, and redirect late arrivals straight to an on-demand replay page or a waitlist for the next cohort.
2. **Contests and Giveaways:** Run strict entry periods without risking disqualifications or angry entrants submitting forms after a promotional window closes.
3. **Post-Campaign Traffic Monetization:** Instead of landing on a dead, boring error screen, expired traffic gets instantly routed to a secondary upsell or your client's core offering.

If your agency builds complex funnels, integrating these timing rules directly into your core assets saves countless hours of backend maintenance. It keeps client campaigns sharp, professional, and entirely hands-off once deployed.
