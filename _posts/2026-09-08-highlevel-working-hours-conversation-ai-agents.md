Managing client expectations when deploying conversational AI has always been a balancing act. You want bots to respond instantly during business hours, but letting an AI loose at 2:00 AM on a Sunday to answer complex service queries can lead to frustrated prospects when the bot inevitably hits a wall. HighLevel has rolled out native Working Hours for Conversation AI agents, solving a massive pain point for agencies setting up automation for local businesses.

### Granular Control Over Bot Schedules

Previously, Conversation AI was largely an all-or-nothing game per channel, or required messy workflow workarounds to toggle agents on and off based on the time of day. Now, you can assign specific working days and hours directly within the agent settings. 

Even better, you aren't forced to apply this globally. You can choose precisely which communication channels respect these hours—like SMS or Instagram DMs—while letting your web chat widget run 24/7. If you manage clients who take split shifts or close early on Wednesdays, you can add multiple time slots per day to match their exact operating reality.

### What Happens After Hours?

An AI that simply goes silent after closing time is a poor experience. HighLevel’s update builds in smart fallback options rather than leaving leads hanging:

* **Off-Hours Auto-Replies:** Send an automated acknowledgment using custom values so the prospect knows they've been heard.
* **Team Notifications:** Alert designated staff members or custom email addresses via email so critical inquiries aren't entirely ignored until morning.
* **Contact Tagging:** Automatically tag after-hours leads, making it effortless to build smart lists for your morning follow-up sequences.
* **Automatic Resume:** If the customer hasn't received a live response by morning, the system can automatically trigger a follow-up when business hours resume.

For agencies utilizing automation to scale client acquisition, these controls bridge the gap between automated speed-to-lead and human touch. You get the benefit of lightning-fast responses without risking bad automated interactions during the middle of the night.

If you are looking to expand what your automated systems can do, [explore HighLevel's AI tools](https://www.gohighlevel.com/ai?fp_ref=affiliatealfa) to see how these scheduling features fit into broader multi-channel client setups.

### Practical Agency Implementation

When configuring this for local business clients, take advantage of the timezone settings. You can run schedules based on the individual contact's local timezone or lock it to the business's physical location. This prevents bots from firing off weirdly timed messages if your client runs multi-region campaigns.

Additionally, ongoing conversations started during working hours will continue smoothly past closing time until the chat goes inactive. This ensures a live dialogue isn't abruptly cut off just because the clock struck 5:00 PM. 

Implementing these schedules takes just a few minutes per agent, drastically cutting down on client complaints about rogue midnight messaging while keeping lead generation humming around the clock.
