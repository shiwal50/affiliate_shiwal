HighLevel has updated its default Conversation AI engine, making GPT-6 Sol the primary model for all new prompt-based agents. If you are building automated chat flows, appointment setters, or support bots for local clients, this shift brings a noticeable jump in accuracy without driving up your token costs.

### What Changes With GPT-6 Sol

Under HighLevel's internal evaluation benchmarks, GPT-6 Sol hits a 98.4% pass rate compared to the 96.3% achieved by GPT-4.1. More importantly, that accuracy boost comes at roughly the same cost per reply. For agency owners managing dozens of client sub-accounts, maintaining predictable margins while improving output quality is a win.

Speed is another factor here. Older models like GPT-5 and GPT-5 Mini have officially been deprecated, with existing agents automatically transitioned to run on GPT-6 Sol. Response generation speeds are now up to 4.5 times faster, cutting down those awkward conversational pauses that make end-users realize they are talking to a bot.

### Practical Implications for Agency Builders

While existing agents keep your previously selected model, any new prompt-based agent defaults to GPT-6 Sol. Because OpenAI is retiring GPT-4.1, you should audit your older agents and manually shift them over to avoid sudden breakage when legacy support ends.

Keep in mind that different language models interpret prompts with subtle variances. When you swap an agent over to GPT-6 Sol, do not just hit save and walk away. Spend five minutes inside the built-in testing interface to run through a few common customer scenarios. Check how it handles edge cases, pricing questions, and booking objections before pushing live.

Leveraging these smarter models can drastically reduce manual intervention for your clients' lead generation pipelines. If you are looking to maximize client retention by automating routine inbound chats, you can [explore HighLevel's AI tools](https://www.gohighlevel.com/ai?fp_ref=affiliatealfa) to see how these conversational agents integrate directly with your calendars and CRM pipelines.

### How to Update Your Agents

Moving your configuration takes just a few clicks:

1. Head over to **AI Agents** and open **Conversation AI**.
2. Select the specific agent you want to update and navigate to the **Build** tab.
3. Open the **Model** dropdown menu.
4. Choose **GPT-6 Sol** (which will be clearly marked as Recommended).
5. Save your changes and test the conversation flow.

As client expectations for instant, accurate digital communication continue to rise, keeping your automated touchpoints sharp is non-negotiable. Take a moment this week to review your active Conversation AI setups and migrate them to the new standard.
