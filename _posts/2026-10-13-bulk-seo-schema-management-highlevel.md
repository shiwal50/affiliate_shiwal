For digital marketing agencies managing dozens of client websites and funnels, technical SEO is a constant maintenance headache. Keeping structured data up to date across hundreds of individual pages usually means diving into the page builder one by one, checking code, and hoping nothing broke during a redesign. HighLevel has finally solved this operational bottleneck by rolling out a centralized SEO and AEO schema dashboard at the website and funnel level.

### What the New Schema Dashboard Does

Instead of hunting through individual pages, you can now navigate to any website or funnel, click into the new SEO & AEO tab, and get an immediate bird’s-eye view of your schema health. 

The interface relies on a clear coverage rating system—categorized as Needs work, Getting there, Good, or Excellent—alongside counts showing which pages are up to date, which might be outdated, and which are missing schema altogether. If you are managing local service businesses, e-commerce stores, or lead generation funnels, this single screen saves hours of manual auditing.

Even better, HighLevel has integrated AI generation directly into this view. You can select multiple pages that lack schema, hit generate, and review the results before publishing anything live. For agencies scaling their SEO service offerings, this removes the tedious grunt work of manual markup generation while keeping human oversight firmly in the loop.

### Practical Implications for Agency Operations

Think about how this changes a standard site audit workflow. Previously, onboarding an existing client meant auditing every single landing page manually to check if local business, service, or product schema was properly implemented. Now, a quick glance at the coverage card instantly highlights the gaps.

Furthermore, the system flags pages as potentially outdated if they have been edited and republished multiple times since the schema was first created. This prevents a common pitfall where a client updates their pricing or address on the page, but the underlying JSON-LD data remains stale. By surfacing these discrepancies automatically, agencies can maintain higher data integrity for Google rich results and AI-driven answer engines.

If you want to leverage automated tagging and advanced optimization for your client portfolios, utilizing [HighLevel's AI tools](https://www.gohighlevel.com/ai?fp_ref=affiliatealfa) can drastically reduce the manual overhead of technical SEO.

### How to Put It to Work Today

There is no complex migration required for this update. Every existing website and funnel in your sub-accounts automatically features the new SEO & AEO tab, and any schema you built previously remains fully intact and synchronized with the page builder.

To clean up a client's site, head over to your Sites tab, open a project, and check the schema coverage card. Filter your view to isolate pages with no schema or those flagged as outdated. Run the bulk generator, review the markup, and publish in a few clicks. It is a straightforward, practical addition that makes enterprise-grade technical SEO management accessible for agencies of any size.
