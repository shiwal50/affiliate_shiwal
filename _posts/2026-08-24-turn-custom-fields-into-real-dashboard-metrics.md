If you manage multiple sub-accounts for clients, you know the frustration of tracking custom numbers. Until recently, if you collected specific numeric data like property values, treatment costs, or monthly software spend through custom contact and opportunity fields, you couldn't actually do much with them natively. They were great for filtering or grouping lists, but if you wanted to see an aggregate sum or an average across a pipeline, you had to export everything to a spreadsheet and run manual calculations.

HighLevel has quietly solved this bottleneck. Numeric and monetary custom fields can now be treated as real, live metrics across dashboard widgets, custom reports, and the custom metric module. 

### How It Works in Practice

The update removes the friction between data collection and client reporting. When you open a dashboard widget or custom report to add a metric, your custom fields now appear right alongside the standard system fields in the dropdown menu. 

Once you select your custom field—say, "Deal Size" or "Estimated Budget"—you can apply standard aggregations:
- **Sum:** Great for total pipeline value based on custom criteria.
- **Average:** Perfect for tracking mean contract sizes per client.
- **Min / Max:** Useful for spotting outliers in high-volume funnels.

Even better, there is zero setup required. The moment you create a new numeric or monetary custom field on a contact or opportunity, it populates automatically into the metric picker. You don't have to map variables or build backend workarounds.

### Why This Changes Client Reporting

For marketing agencies, client retention often comes down to clear proof of ROI. If your clients operate in niche industries—like real estate, medical clinics, or B2B SaaS—their key performance indicators rarely fit neatly into standard CRM metrics.

Instead of building clumsy multi-step workflows just to push data points into external data studio dashboards, your team can now build clean, native reporting views directly inside the client's sub-account. It keeps clients inside your white-labeled environment and saves hours of manual data wrangling every month.

If you have been stitching together spreadsheets just to show clients the aggregate financial impact of your campaigns, it is time to audit your custom fields and pull them directly into your reporting views.
