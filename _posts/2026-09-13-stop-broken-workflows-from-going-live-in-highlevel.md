If you manage client automations at scale, you have experienced the dread of pushing a workflow live only to find out a misconfigured webhook, missing custom field, or broken branching logic quietly stalled half your database. Debugging post-launch errors wastes hours and damages trust with local business clients who rely on your agency for lead flow.

HighLevel just shipped a major update to how automations are validated and deployed. Instead of letting broken logic slip silently into production, the platform now forces a hard stop at the publish phase when configuration issues are detected. 

### One Unified Error Sidebar

Previously, checking for workflow errors felt like a scavenger hunt. Frontend warnings and backend execution issues lived in separate corners of the UI, often showing conflicting information or no warning at all until a lead hit a dead end. 

Now, all configuration errors merge into a single **Highlight Error sidebar**. When you click Publish on a faulty workflow, a modal stops you in your tracks and lists every unresolved issue. From there, you can open a dedicated sidebar that points directly to the problem area. 

Even better, you are given two direct paths to resolution for each flagged error:
1. **Open Step:** Jumps your view straight to the offending action or trigger so you can inspect it.
2. **AI Assist:** Pulls in AI recommendations to help resolve the misconfiguration on the spot.

### Save Your Work Without Fixing Everything Right Away

One of the most frustrating quirks of the old workflow builder was getting blocked from saving your progress simply because a secondary branch contained an unconfigured step. You were forced to either delete half-built logic or hack together placeholder settings just to save your canvas.

HighLevel has decoupled validation from the save function. You can now manually or auto-save your workflow canvas even when errors exist. If you need to pause mid-build, step away for lunch, or hand the project to a colleague, your work stays intact. The safety gate only triggers when you hit **Publish**, ensuring that half-baked sequences never reach your live infrastructure.

### Why This Matters for Agency Operations

For agency owners and operations managers, this update significantly reduces QA overhead. When you onboard new virtual assistants or junior automation builders, you want guardrails that prevent them from pushing broken logic to a live client account. 

This validation gate acts as a final line of defense, ensuring that your team's delivery standards remain high without requiring manual checklist audits for every single trigger and action. Combined with [HighLevel's robust automation and AI tools](https://www.gohighlevel.com/?fp_ref=affiliatealfa), these kinds of structural updates make it much easier to scale client delivery without sacrificing reliability.

### How to Leverage It Today

Open up any complex multi-branch workflow in your sub-account and try hitting publish on an intentionally broken sequence. Test out the new error sidebar to see how quickly you can jump between broken nodes. By making validation an active part of the publishing workflow rather than a passive hope, you can eliminate silent failures and keep your agency's lead generation engines running smoothly.
