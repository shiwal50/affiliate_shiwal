If your agency runs client communities or private masterminds inside HighLevel, scaling up usually introduces a painful bottleneck: manually reviewing join requests. When you use membership questions to vet applicants, someone has to read through every submission to weed out low-effort answers, spam, or poor fits. HighLevel just solved this by adding a "Requested to Join Group" workflow trigger paired with native AI screening capabilities.

### What the New Workflow Trigger Changes

Previously, managing community access meant manual reviews or letting anyone in blindly. With this update, whenever a user requests access to a group, it fires a workflow. Crucially, HighLevel makes membership question responses dynamically available as filter fields. 

This means you aren't just automating an approval notification; you are automating the qualification process itself. You can evaluate the actual text submitted by the applicant before deciding whether to grant access.

### How to Build an AI-Screened Join Flow

Setting this up takes just a few minutes inside the workflow builder:

1. **The Trigger:** Select "Requested to Join Group" and choose your target community.
2. **The Evaluation:** Pass the user's membership question responses directly into a GPT prompt action within the workflow. Instruct the AI to analyze the text for quality, relevance, or potential spam.
3. **The Logic:** Use an If/Else condition based on the GPT output. If the response passes your criteria, automatically execute the "Grant Group Access" action, add a tag, and fire off a welcome sequence. If it fails, route the contact for manual review or send a polite rejection.

This setup shifts community management from a tedious administrative chore into a background process that runs seamlessly 24/7.
