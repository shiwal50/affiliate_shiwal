HighLevel has rolled out a major update for AI Studio, introducing native live chat and email chat widgets that integrate directly into your custom applications. Instead of relying on generic third-party chat bubbles or awkwardly bolted-on communication tools, you can now generate fully styled, native chat modules simply by prompting AI Studio.

For agency owners building custom client portals, landing pages, or functional mini-apps using AI tools, this update bridges a critical gap. Previously, embedding a way for users to communicate meant juggling separate widget scripts, managing external styling conflicts, and dealing with fragmented lead data. Now, the chat widget is built directly into your app's code and inherits your site's exact colors, fonts, and layout.

### Why This Matters for Client Delivery

When you deploy custom React or TanStack Start projects for clients through AI Studio, client interaction is everything. This native implementation means visitors can drop files, pick up past conversation histories seamlessly upon returning, and trigger automated security captchas without breaking a sweat.

More importantly, the underlying mechanics tie straight into your core HighLevel setup. Every message lands right in your standard Conversations tab. Business hours, out-of-hours auto-responses, inactivity timers, and satisfaction ratings all pull directly from your account's established widget settings. There is no redundant configuration required.

Agency teams can also track performance using the new dedicated analytics suite. By navigating to More, Analytics, and Chat Widgets inside AI Studio, you get a clean breakdown of chat loads, open rates, visitor feedback, and a unified table of every lead generated through the interface. You can filter by date range, export data, and pinpoint exactly which page initiated the conversation.
