HighLevel has just rolled out one of the platform's most requested feature updates: conditional logic and score-based redirects for Quizzes. Until now, building an interactive assessment on your client sites meant dropping users onto a generic results page regardless of their answers. While standard forms and surveys have enjoyed flexible routing for a while, quizzes remained a statistical dead end.

That limitation is officially gone. You can now dynamically route respondents to completely different URLs based on their individual answers, overall score, or category-specific performance. For marketing agencies building lead generation engines, this opens up a massive opportunity to deliver true personalization at scale without resorting to clunky external quiz plugins.

### What the New Quiz Logic Actually Delivers

The update introduces the 'Redirect to URL' conditional logic type into the Quiz builder, paired with four new score-based fields:

* **Overall Score:** Route users based on their total cumulative percentage or points.
* **Category Score:** Target specific thresholds within isolated topics.
* **Highest Category:** Automatically detect a respondent's strongest area and send them down a tailored path.
* **Lowest Category:** Pinpoint their biggest weakness so you can immediately serve up remedial resources or corrective product offers.

Instead of displaying a simple static text summary, a quiz can now act as a dynamic triage tool. If you are running digital marketing audits for local businesses, a low score in SEO can instantly push them to a specialized checkout page for your agency's monthly search optimization retainer. If they score high, you can route them straight to an advanced booking calendar for a high-ticket strategy call.

### Practical Agency Use Case: Automated Product Recommendations

Imagine you manage social media accounts for an e-commerce brand selling fitness supplements. You build a 10-question quiz titled 'Find Your Ideal Supplement Stack.' 

Previously, every user saw the same generic recommendation screen. Now, using Highest Category conditions, users whose answers index highest in weight loss get redirected to a custom landing page featuring your fat-burner bundles. Users whose scores point toward muscle building get sent straight to your protein and creatine funnels.

Because these rules integrate natively with [HighLevel's advanced AI and automation tools](https://www.gohighlevel.com/ai?fp_ref=affiliatealfa), you aren't just changing a URL. The moment the redirect fires, the contact record updates, tags are applied based on their quiz outcome, and subsequent email sequences or SMS follow-ups automatically match the exact product category they were routed to.

### Setting Up Your First Smart Quiz

Configuring these journeys takes just a few minutes inside your sub-account. Open up your Quiz builder, navigate to the conditional logic settings, and choose the 'Redirect to URL' action. From there, layer in your score parameters or specific answer combinations and define the destination web address.

HighLevel has noted that additional conditional logic types—such as displaying custom messages or disqualifying leads directly within the flow—are currently on the roadmap. For now, mastering URL redirects gives agencies an immediate edge in turning static traffic into highly segmented, conversion-ready prospects.
