If you run a marketing agency, you know the drill. You spin up a new sub-account for a client, hand over the login credentials, and immediately get a text asking why they are seeing pipeline values and metrics from other campaigns or why the numbers don't match what they expect. General widgets on sub-account dashboards have historically been a bit too generic, showing a broad view of data that often required manual explanation or filtering every single time a user logged in.

HighLevel just rolled out a major update to sub-account dashboards that fixes this exact friction point. You can now configure, filter, rename, theme, and duplicate general widgets directly. For agency owners managing dozens or hundreds of clients, this is a massive win for client onboarding and perceived professionalism.

### Permanent Filters Save Your Sanity

The standout feature here is the new Conditions tab. Instead of relying on universal dashboard filters that users have to toggle, you can now bake permanent filter conditions right into individual widgets. 

For example, if you have a sales rep or a local business client who only cares about their own leads, you can set `Assigned user = Logged in user` once. From that moment on, every time that specific team member opens the dashboard, the widget automatically scopes down to their performance. No extra clicks, no confusion.

You can also lock down widgets by tags, sources, lead values, custom fields, and attribution models. If you want a widget to permanently track a specific paid ads campaign source without mixing in organic traffic, you set the condition once and it persists forever.

### Date Overrides and Year-over-Year Comparisons

Another common headache has been the disconnect between global date filters and specific metrics you want to highlight. Now, you can apply a date range override to individual widgets. 

If you want a particular KPI card to always show year-to-date numbers while the rest of the dashboard flexes on a monthly view, you can lock that specific card down. Even better, HighLevel added a comparison mode directly to the widget face. You can compare the current period against the previous period or last year with a couple of clicks, giving your clients an instant view of their growth story without needing a custom report built from scratch.

To make things even clearer, the date property and active date range now display directly on the face of the widget. When a client asks why two metrics look different, the answer is right there in plain sight.

### Turning Generic Data into Client-Ready Real Estate

Dashboards should look like they belong to the brand using them, not like a generic out-of-the-box CRM view. With the latest updates, you can rename widget titles to use client-friendly terminology, apply visual themes that match their brand guidelines, and duplicate widgets to segment data cleanly.

If you are scaling your agency and want to reduce client churn by delivering cleaner, more intuitive reporting, these dashboard upgrades make it much easier to build sticky setups.
