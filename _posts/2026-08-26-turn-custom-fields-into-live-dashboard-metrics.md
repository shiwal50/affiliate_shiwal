If you manage client sub-accounts, you know the pain of trying to build meaningful reports around specific business data. Until now, custom fields on contacts and opportunities were mostly useful for storing data, filtering lists, or segmenting smart lists. If you wanted to see the actual financial sum of a custom property value field or calculate the average deal size based on a custom numeric field, you had to export your data into a spreadsheet and do the math manually.

HighLevel has quietly solved this bottleneck. Numeric and monetary custom fields can now be used as actual metrics across sub-account dashboards, custom reports, and the Custom Metric module. 

### How It Works in Practice

When you open up a dashboard widget or custom report to add a metric, your custom fields now appear right alongside the standard system fields. You aren't forced to use rigid built-in parameters anymore. Instead, you can pull in any custom numeric or monetary field you've created—whether it tracks treatment costs, property values, equipment rentals, or project budgets—and apply an aggregation rule.

You have four core aggregation options to choose from:
- **Sum:** Great for total revenue, cumulative project values, or aggregate pipeline volume.
- **Average:** Perfect for tracking average deal sizes, mean patient spend, or typical loan amounts.
- **Min / Max:** Ideal for identifying outliers, highest-value clients, or lowest-cost service tiers.

Even better, there is zero setup required. The moment you create a new custom field in your CRM, it becomes metric-ready automatically. 

### Why This Changes Agency Reporting

For marketing agencies, reporting is often the difference between a client staying for a year or churning out after month three. Clients don't just want to see raw lead counts or form submissions; they want to see revenue impact and pipeline health tailored to their specific vertical.

Previously, proving ROI meant pulling weekly CSV exports and building custom charts in external BI tools just to show aggregate custom metrics. Now, you can build client-facing dashboards directly inside [HighLevel](https://www.gohighlevel.com/?fp_ref=affiliatealfa) that reflect the exact metrics their sales teams care about.

Imagine running a real estate sub-account where every opportunity has a custom field for "Estimated Commission." You can now drop a dashboard widget on the home screen that sums up total projected commission in real-time, filtered by pipeline stage. Or picture a medical clinic tracking patient lifetime value through a custom numeric field, displayed cleanly as an average on their custom report.

### Getting Started

Take a look at your current sub-account templates. Identify the custom numeric and monetary fields you're already collecting via forms, surveys, or manual entry. Head over to your dashboard widgets or custom reports, swap out generic placeholders for your custom fields, and apply the appropriate aggregation.
