If you run a marketing agency with dozens or hundreds of sub-accounts, you already know the pain of client reporting. Historically, setting up agency dashboards meant cloning templates and manually updating location-specific details, or worse, building custom reports from scratch for every single client. HighLevel has finally solved this bottleneck by adding custom values and system tokens directly into dashboard and report widgets.

### The Multi-Location Reporting Headache

When managing a portfolio of local businesses or franchise locations, uniformity is key. You want every client to log into a polished, branded dashboard that shows their specific metrics, addresses, and tracking numbers. Previously, if you wanted to embed an external report URL dynamically or personalize a widget title based on who was looking at it, you hit a wall.

That meant agencies had two bad choices: maintain separate dashboard templates for every location, or build generic dashboards that lacked personal context. Neither option scales well when you are trying to grow your agency without endlessly expanding your operations team.

### What the Custom Values Update Changes

HighLevel's new `{}` picker allows you to inject location fields, user details, and your custom values directly into dashboard elements. We are talking about dynamic text boxes, titles, embed URLs, and even widget conditions.

Imagine building a single master dashboard template for your entire client base. When a client in Austin logs in, the dashboard pulls their specific location name, phone number, and address automatically. When a client in Chicago logs in, the exact same dashboard adapts instantly to show their details. No duplication. No manual text updates.

Even better, this works for dynamic embed URLs. If you pull in external reporting tools, BI software, or custom client portals via an iframe, you can now pass the location ID or custom value straight into the URL string. Each sub-account automatically loads its own isolated dataset without requiring a separate layout.

### Practical Implementation for Agencies

To put this into practice, open up your master dashboard template in Edit mode. Instead of hardcoding text into your widget titles or text elements, click the new `{}` button next to the input field. You can pull from standard system tokens like location name, email, and timezone, or dig into your custom values organized by folder under your settings.

If you want to test out new operational efficiencies like this across your tech stack, you can [explore HighLevel's platform](https://www.gohighlevel.com/?fp_ref=affiliatealfa) to see how deep these multi-location capabilities go. By standardizing your reporting layers into a single template, you eliminate hours of repetitive onboarding setup and give your clients a cleaner, more personalized view of their campaign performance.

Custom values resolve dynamically as soon as the user switches from edit mode to view mode. If a token happens to be blank for a specific sub-account, it simply renders clean space, keeping your interface professional and error-free. It is a subtle update that fundamentally changes how agencies handle scale across multiple client portals.
