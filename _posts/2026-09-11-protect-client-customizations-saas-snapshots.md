If you run an agency on HighLevel using SaaS mode, you have likely felt the cold sweat of deploying a snapshot update to an active sub-account. Until now, pushing a global snapshot template out to client accounts meant crossing your fingers and hoping it didn’t overwrite the custom funnels, unique landing pages, or tailored workflows your clients built for themselves. 

Silent overrides are a massive headache for scaling agencies. When an update accidentally wipes out a client's hard work, it erodes trust and generates support tickets that eat up your team's valuable time. HighLevel has finally solved this friction point by introducing automated conflict detection for individual SaaS sub-accounts.

### How Snapshot Protection Works

When you enable SaaS for a sub-account and attach a snapshot, the system now scans for potential conflicts. If the incoming snapshot assets match or overlap with elements a client has already customized, the platform stops in its tracks. Instead of a destructive silent overwrite, you are presented with a clear warning prompt.

From there, you or your client retain total control. You can evaluate the conflict and explicitly choose whether to preserve the existing custom assets or let the new snapshot version take over. If no conflicts exist, the snapshot applies smoothly just like it always did. 

This update applies directly to both SaaS V1 and V2 accounts. For bulk SaaS setups where individual prompting isn't yet practical, HighLevel has baked in a safe default behavior that automatically protects existing assets behind the scenes without risk of data loss. Additional updates are also in the pipeline to bring full conflict warning capabilities to bulk updates soon.

### Why This Matters for Agency Operations

For agency owners managing dozens or hundreds of client portals, confidence in deployment is everything. Being able to push updates across your ecosystem without fear of breaking live client configurations changes how frequently you can roll out new marketing assets, forms, and automations.

If you have been hesitant to fully automate your client onboarding or hesitate to push template updates due to fear of collateral damage, this change removes that operational bottleneck. It allows you to leverage [HighLevel's white-label SaaS features](https://www.gohighlevel.com/protrial?fp_ref=affiliatealfa) with far greater peace of mind, knowing your clients' unique adjustments are secure.

### Best Practices for Your Agency

Even with automated warnings in place, keeping your snapshots organized remains a best practice. Try to maintain a clear boundary between your core global assets—like base automation sequences and default pipelines—and the specific pages or funnels you expect clients to personalize.

When deploying updates to high-value accounts, communicate with your clients about upcoming template additions so they understand what is changing. By combining HighLevel's new safety checks with a solid deployment workflow, you can keep your client environments standardized while keeping their bespoke work entirely safe.
