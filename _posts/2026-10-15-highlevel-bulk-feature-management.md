If you manage a large portfolio of client sub-accounts, you already know the pain of rolling out a new tool or restricting features. Until now, standardizing setups meant manually clicking into individual accounts, hunting through settings, and toggling permissions one by one. For agencies running on [HighLevel's SaaS mode](https://www.gohighlevel.com/protrial?fp_ref=affiliatealfa), this repetitive admin work ate up hours of valuable team time every time a tiered packaging update rolled out.

HighLevel has finally solved this bottleneck by introducing bulk feature management on the Sub-Accounts page. You can now select multiple sub-accounts, click a single update button, and push your desired feature configurations across all of them in one go.

### How the Bulk Update Works

The implementation is straightforward and built directly into the agency dashboard. Navigate to your Sub-Accounts listing, check the boxes for the locations you want to modify, and select the new update feature option. From there, a clear interface lets you search for specific features, pick entire feature groups, or apply sweeping 'Enable all' and 'Disable all' commands.

Once you define your changes, the platform processes the request in the background. Because these actions are queued, you don't have to sit and watch a loading bar. Most batches finish applying within two to five minutes, and you can track the exact progress of every request straight from the Bulk Actions History log. If you accidentally include a sub-account or a feature you didn't mean to touch, the system allows you to refine your list before final execution.

### Practical Implications for Agency Operators

This update changes how agencies handle tiered service offerings and compliance rollouts. Imagine you launch a new SMS compliance feature or an advanced AI workflow tool that you only want available to clients on your highest monthly retainer. Previously, provisioning that feature to fifty different client locations was a multi-hour chore prone to human error—meaning some clients might accidentally get access to premium tools they didn't pay for.

Now, you can segment your sub-accounts, apply precise feature restrictions instantly, and ensure your packaging tiers remain strictly enforced. It also simplifies onboarding. When you spin up a batch of new client accounts for a local lead-gen campaign, you can instantly standardize their core toolsets without manual verification.

For growing agencies scaling their operations, eliminating friction in account management is non-negotiable. This update removes a persistent operational headache, letting your team focus on client results rather than backend configuration checkboxes. If you haven't explored how centralized controls can streamline your agency infrastructure, it is worth testing out these new bulk actions on your client roster today.
