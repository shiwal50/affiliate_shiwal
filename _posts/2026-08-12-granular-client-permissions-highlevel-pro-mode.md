If you run an agency on HighLevel's SaaS mode, managing client expectations usually comes down to what they can see inside their sub-accounts. Give them too much access, and they break workflows or get overwhelmed. Give them too little, and your support queue fills up with unnecessary requests. 

A recent update from the HighLevel product team makes this balancing act much easier by overhauling sub-account permissions. Instead of wrestling with a vague, flat list of thirty or so toggles, you now have access to over 80 distinct feature controls organized into a clean, three-tier hierarchy: Category, Module, and Feature.

### Why the New Permission Structure Matters for Agencies

When you white-label a CRM and resell it as your own proprietary software, perception is everything. Clients expect a streamlined experience tailored to their exact subscription tier. Previously, disabling certain tools could be frustratingly imprecise. You might turn off a feature globally, only to find a back-door workaround or an exposed menu item still visible in a client's dashboard.

The new Category > Module > Feature breakdown lets you slice access with surgical precision. Want to grant access to reporting modules while hiding advanced workflow builder components? You can do that. Need to package a specific set of features exclusively for higher-tier SaaS clients while stripping them from your entry-level plans? The hierarchy maps directly onto your SaaS plan configurations.

### Practical Implementation: Structuring Your SaaS Tiers

This update fundamentally changes how you can package your agency offerings. Let's look at how to use these granular controls practically:

1. **The Core Tier:** Keep foundational CRM tools, conversation inboxes, and basic pipeline management visible. Turn off complex automation builders, custom code injections, and advanced reporting customisation to prevent accidental damage.
2. **The Growth Tier:** Introduce automated workflows, basic review management, and social planners. This tier is built for clients who are actively scaling their marketing but don't need root-level system administration.
3. **The Elite Tier:** Unlock everything. Power users get full visibility into custom values, advanced triggers, and integrations.

Because these settings integrate directly into SaaS plan management—which is built natively into [HighLevel's SaaS Pro plan](https://www.gohighlevel.com/protrial?fp_ref=affiliatealfa)—you can automate the provisioning process. When a client upgrades their subscription through your billing portal, their feature set expands instantly without manual intervention from your team.
