If you are running a restaurant vertical inside your agency, you know how critical friction-free online ordering is. Every extra click between a hungry customer and a completed cart results in abandoned checkouts and lost revenue. HighLevel’s latest update to its Founder's Plan Restaurant system tackles this exact bottleneck by rolling out flexible guest checkout controls alongside centralized staff order entry.

Previously, forcing account creation during online ordering or table booking created a massive barrier for first-time diners. People just want to order food, pay, and move on with their evening. They do not want to verify an email, set up a password, or remember another login for a local spot they might only visit once a month.

### Granular Control Over Guest Sign-Ins

With the new update found under `Restaurants → Restaurant Website → Setup and sharing`, agencies can now configure sign-in requirements globally for both online ordering and table reservations. You have three distinct options to choose from depending on your client's operational model:

1. **Never Ask:** Eliminates account creation entirely. The checkout screen simply collects the diner's name, mobile number, and email address. Perfect for quick-service spots where speed is everything.
2. **Offer It:** Gives customers the option to sign in for a faster checkout next time, but lets them bypass it smoothly if they are in a rush.
3. **Require It:** Best for loyalty-heavy establishments where member-only perks or stored payment profiles are standard practice.

### Centralized Staff Order Entry

In addition to the guest checkout improvements, the platform has introduced a unified **New Order** interface for staff. Phone orders, counter walk-ins, and complex dine-in requests now live on the exact same screen as online delivery and pickup tickets.

Instead of managing a fragmented workflow where phone orders get scribbled on paper pads and entered later, front-of-house staff can immediately select dine-in tables, build multi-round orders with specific kitchen notes, or process delivery distances and driver notes directly from the CRM. Saved customer addresses map automatically to profiles like Home, Work, or Other, drastically speeding up repeat call-ins.
