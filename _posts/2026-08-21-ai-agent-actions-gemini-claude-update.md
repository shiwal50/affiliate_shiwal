HighLevel has just expanded its workflow AI capabilities by introducing Anthropic and Google models directly into the AI Agent Action. Previously, building automated logic meant relying solely on OpenAI. Now, agencies have direct access to a broader ecosystem of language models, including Claude and Gemini variants, right inside the standard automation builder.

### What Changed in the AI Agent Action

The update brings a redesigned model picker that groups options neatly by provider. Alongside this layout change, HighLevel has introduced a thinking chip indicator for models that support advanced reasoning, complete with descriptions to help you evaluate options before saving.

More importantly, you can now control reasoning effort directly from the dropdown. Setting effort to Low, Medium, or High lets you tune how deeply the model processes complex queries. This opens up clear operational efficiencies for marketing agencies handling heavy client workflows.

### A Practical Agency Use Case

Think about how your agency handles incoming leads. A heavy, flagship model like Claude Opus or GPT-5.6 can handle intricate multi-step reasoning where conversational nuance and strict accuracy are mandatory. But what about simple inbound tasks, like checking if a contact wants a morning or afternoon appointment?

Using a massive model for basic intent classification is a waste of execution time and tokens. With this update, you can route straightforward tasks to faster, lighter models while saving your heavy-reasoning setups for complex sales qualifying paths. Layered on top of recent token optimizations, routing tasks efficiently can significantly cut your operational overhead.

### How Effort Control Changes Workflow Design

When you build an AI agent to draft customized email replies based on CRM data, you want quality. Turning the effort setting to High gives the model room to deliberate before generating a response. For high-volume, rapid-fire tag sorting or field updates, dropping the effort level down keeps automation snappy and prevents latency bottlenecks.

If you want to experiment with how these advanced capabilities can overhaul your client delivery, [explore HighLevel's AI tools](https://www.gohighlevel.com/ai?fp_ref=affiliatealfa) to see what's possible.

### Implementation Steps

1. Open any active workflow containing an AI Agent Action.
2. Click into the model selection dropdown.
3. Browse the grouped providers (Anthropic, Google, and OpenAI) and review the thinking chips.
4. Select an appropriate reasoning effort level (Low, Medium, or High) based on task complexity.
5. Save and publish your changes.

This update shifts the workflow builder from a one-size-fits-all AI approach to a modular framework where you can pair the exact right intelligence level to the exact right task. For agencies scaling automated operations, that level of granular control is a welcome addition.
