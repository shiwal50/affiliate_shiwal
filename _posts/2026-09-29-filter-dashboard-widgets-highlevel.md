If you manage multiple client sub-accounts in HighLevel, you know the pain of the default agency dashboard. Every time a client logs in, they see a generic overview filled with data that might not apply to them, or worse, metrics belonging to other team members. Until now, fixing this meant building custom external reports or constantly fielding questions about messy numbers.

HighLevel has finally updated sub-account dashboards by making general widgets fully editable. You can now configure permanent filter conditions, lock down custom date ranges, and apply comparison modes directly on the widget face. 

### What You Can Configure Now

The update introduces a few specific tabs when you edit a general widget from the dashboard view:

* **Conditions Tab:** Apply permanent data filters based on assigned users, tags, sources, lead values, custom fields, and attribution models. Once set, these filters persist every time the dashboard loads.
* **Date Range Overrides:** Lock a specific widget to a custom time frame—like "This Year" compared against "Last Year"—independent of the global dashboard date filter.
* **Explicit Date Labels:** The widget face now explicitly displays the date property and range it calculates. No more guessing why two similar blocks show different totals.
* **Visual Themes & Renaming:** Clean up technical property names into client-friendly language and apply visual styles that match your branding.

### How This Solves Real Agency Headaches

The biggest win here is permanent user-level scoping. If you have a multi-person sales team working inside a single sub-account, you used to have to rely on manual filters during every session. By setting a permanent condition where the assigned user equals the logged-in user, every staff member automatically sees only their own pipeline and lead metrics upon login.

For client-facing portals, this turns a generic workspace into a bespoke reporting tool. You can duplicate a core widget four times, filter each one for a different traffic source or campaign tag, and give your client a clean breakdown without them ever needing to touch a filter dropdown.

### Getting Started

To update your views, head over to your sub-account dashboard, click **Edit dashboard** in the top right, and hover over any general widget to access the three-dot menu. From there, select **Edit** to start locking down your conditions and date ranges.
