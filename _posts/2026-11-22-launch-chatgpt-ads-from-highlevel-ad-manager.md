If your marketing agency manages paid traffic, you already know how fragmented reporting can get when juggling Meta, Google, and LinkedIn. HighLevel is changing the game by adding OpenAI as a native platform inside Ad Manager. You can now build, target, and track ChatGPT ads without ever leaving your sub-account dashboard.

### What OpenAI Ads Mean for Agencies

People are shifting their initial research phase away from traditional search engines and directly into conversational AI. By placing ads inside ChatGPT apps on iOS and Android, as well as desktop and mobile web interfaces, you get in front of prospective clients right at the moment of intent. 

Instead of relying strictly on traditional keyword targeting, OpenAI ad placements use context hints. You describe the exact situation or problem your prospective customer is facing—in plain language—and the system places your ad accordingly. HighLevel even generates suggested hints for you with a single click.

### Familiar Workflow, New Channel

You do not need to retrain your media buying team on a completely foreign interface. The campaign structure follows the standard format you already use for other channels:

* **Campaign Level:** Set your objectives (Reach, Clicks, or Conversions), daily or lifetime budgets, scheduling, and location targeting.
* **Ad Group Level:** Configure manual Max CPC or Maximize results bidding, along with your destination URLs and context hints.
* **Ad Creative Level:** Drop in your 50-character title, 100-character description, landing page URL, and square image while watching a live ChatGPT preview update in real-time.

You can also feed custom audiences into your campaigns by uploading customer lists or pulling directly from your HighLevel smart lists. Phone numbers and emails are securely hashed before reaching OpenAI, ensuring data privacy standards are met.

### Unified Reporting and Analytics

One of the biggest headaches for agency owners is consolidating data across multiple client dashboards. With this update, OpenAI ad spend, clicks, and conversions sit right alongside your other channels in HighLevel Statistics. You can filter by platform to view ChatGPT campaigns in isolation or compare them side-by-side with your Google and Meta performance.
