If you manage multiple sub-accounts for clients, you know the pain of the generic dashboard. Every time a client logs in, they see a sprawling mass of data that includes metrics they didn't ask for, team-wide numbers they shouldn't be tracking, or date ranges that require constant manual tweaking. It forces agency owners to waste hours trying to explain why a widget shows total agency pipeline instead of just their localized campaign performance.

HighLevel just rolled out a major update to sub-account dashboards that fixes this exact bottleneck. You can now edit, filter, theme, and duplicate general widgets directly inside your sub-accounts. For agency owners managing client retention, this is a massive win.

### Permanent Filters Change the Game

The standout feature here is the new Conditions tab. Previously, if you wanted a widget to show only a specific user's deals or leads tagged with a certain campaign, you were out of luck unless you built custom reporting pages. Now, you can apply permanent filter conditions based on assigned users, tags, sources, lead values, custom fields, and attribution.

Think about what this means for a multi-person client team. You can set the assigned user condition to the logged-in user once. When the sales rep logs in, they see only their pipeline. When the sales manager logs in, they see the team's overall numbers. You no longer need to maintain separate dashboards for different tiers of users within the same sub-account.

### Lock Down Your Date Ranges

Another subtle frustration has always been the global date filter. Change the dashboard view to check a specific campaign last month, and suddenly your high-level KPI widgets shift too, confusing clients who are used to looking at a rolling year-to-date view.

With the latest update, you can set a date range override for individual widgets under Advanced Settings. You can lock a widget to "This Year" with a year-over-year comparison against last year. The widget face now explicitly displays the date property and active date range, completely eliminating the "why do these two numbers not match?" support tickets from confused clients.

### Building Client-Ready Snapshots

When combined with HighLevel's snapshot system, this update lets you template truly customized reporting environments. You can rename widgets to match your client's internal vocabulary, apply visual themes that match their brand colors, and duplicate widgets to segment data cleanly.

If you are scaling an agency, efficiency lives in the setup phase. By configuring these widgets once inside a template sub-account, you push out polished, client-ready dashboards automatically with every new onboarding. It turns a generic CRM into an executive-level reporting tool that clients actually understand.
