Meta has officially drawn a hard line in the sand for WhatsApp marketing. The legacy Cloud API is effectively frozen for marketing sends and will completely stop delivering promotional messages on October 13, 2027. If your agency manages client sub-accounts running broadcast campaigns or automated promotional sequences through HighLevel, you have a roughly 12-month runway to transition everything over to Meta's Marketing Messages API—known inside HighLevel as MM Lite.

While October 2027 sounds distant, waiting until the deadline is a tactical mistake. The reality is that your clients' current campaigns are running without critical delivery optimizations, granular conversion tracking, and modern creative formats that are exclusively tied to MM Lite. Meta's own internal testing across millions of messages revealed up to a 9% delivery lift for high-engagement sends simply by utilizing the newer API's engagement-based routing signals.

### What Changes When You Enable MM Lite

Transitioning a sub-account doesn't mean rebuilding your client setups from scratch. Your existing phone numbers and approved marketing templates carry over seamlessly without needing re-approval from Meta. Service and utility messages continue functioning on the standard Cloud API exactly as they always have—this mandate impacts promotional and marketing sends exclusively.

However, unlocking MM Lite gives your agency immediate access to capabilities the Cloud API will never receive:

* **Advanced Conversion Tracking:** You can finally track the full buyer journey from the initial WhatsApp message straight through to a completed purchase, moving far beyond basic delivery and read receipts.
* **Native Creative Formats:** Support for animated GIF headers in templates and Android app deep links that open your client's mobile app directly from the chat.
* **Richer Metrics:** Get clear visibility into spend per template, cost per click, and cost per delivery to prove tangible ROI to your retainer clients.

### How to Handle the Migration Without Client Friction

For growing agencies managing dozens or hundreds of sub-accounts, mass migrations can feel daunting. The key is to prioritize your accounts by volume. Start by enabling MM Lite on your highest-volume messaging sub-accounts first. They will capture the immediate delivery lifts and improved revenue attribution, proving the value of the upgrade early.

The process itself takes just minutes per account. Inside the sub-account, navigate to **Settings > WhatsApp**, click the banner to enable MM Lite, and approve the requested permissions through the Meta Business interface.
