Managing client pipelines often turns messy when sales reps are forced to sift through endless, irrelevant fields. HighLevel has just rolled out conditional custom fields for opportunities in Labs, letting agency admins show or hide fields and folders—and enforce mandatory inputs—based on specific pipeline stages, statuses, or dropdown selections.

### Why Dynamic Opportunity Forms Matter

Previously, creating custom fields meant every user saw every field, regardless of where a deal sat in the sales funnel. This inevitably led to cluttered layouts, skipped data entry, and dirty CRM data that broke downstream reporting. 

With this new update, your pipeline forms can finally adapt in real time. For instance, if a deal moves to a "Proposal Sent" stage, you can automatically trigger mandatory fields for budget size and contract length. If an opportunity is marked "Lost," you can require a specific loss-reason dropdown before the deal can be closed.

### Practical Implementation for Client Sub-Accounts

To put this into practice, navigate to your agency sub-account settings, enable the feature under Labs, and head over to your custom fields settings to establish your conditional logic rules. You can use single conditions or complex AND/OR logic relying on pipelines, stages, and multi-select fields. 

If you are managing clients who run complex sales processes—such as enterprise B2B service providers or multi-location franchises—this feature eliminates human error by preventing reps from saving opportunities with incomplete information. It keeps your workspace lean, ensures data consistency across your team, and dramatically improves the accuracy of your forecasting dashboards.

Agencies looking to optimize their internal setups or deliver a more tailored experience to demanding enterprise clients should test these rules immediately.
