Agency billing has historically meant choosing between rigid, generic templates or spending hours wrestling with custom code to match a client's brand standards. If you run a marketing agency handling recurring retainers, project fees, or ad spend pass-throughs, clean and professional invoices are part of the client experience. HighLevel has addressed this friction by rolling out a new Layout AI Builder, allowing you to generate and refine custom invoice designs entirely through natural language prompts.

### Moving Beyond Rigid Templates

Previously, customizing an invoice layout in the CRM meant working within strict platform constraints. If a client needed specific line item structures, alternative tax displays, or bank routing instructions placed front and center, you often had to compromise. 

Now, you can simply open the Layout AI Builder under your Payments and Invoices settings, describe what you need, and let the system handle the heavy lifting. For example, you can type a prompt like: *"A clean two-column invoice with my agency logo top-left, an itemized table in the center, and payment terms and bank details at the bottom."* Alternatively, if you are migrating an existing layout from another software, you can upload a reference or describe your current format to have AI recreate it instantly.

### Practical Implications for Agency Operations

What makes this update genuinely useful for agencies is the balance between automated generation and granular visual control. You are never locked into the first output the AI produces. You can ask for iterative adjustments through chat, or drop straight into the container to manually tweak padding, alignment, colors, and content elements.

Every iteration creates a secure restore point. If an adjustment goes sideways, you can roll back to any previous version instantly with a single click or keyboard shortcut. Once you publish a layout, it locks safely once an invoice is sent or partially paid, protecting your historical financial records from accidental post-send edits. If you issue draft invoices using a layout that gets deleted later, the system gracefully falls back to the default template without throwing errors.

### How to Activate It

Because this feature is currently rolling out under experimental settings, you will need to enable it manually before your team can start designing. Navigate to Settings, open Labs under Sub-Account features, and locate **AI-Powered Custom Invoice Layouts**. From there, you can activate the feature for individual sub-accounts or roll it out globally across your entire agency architecture.
