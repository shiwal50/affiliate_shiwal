If you run an agency on HighLevel, you know the dual-edged sword of snapshots. They are the ultimate scaling engine—allowing you to spin up a fully built-out sub-account with funnels, workflows, and calendars in a matter of clicks. But they have historically carried a silent risk: if a sub-account owner made bespoke tweaks to their funnels or websites, pushing an updated snapshot could inadvertently wipe out those custom assets without leaving a trail.

HighLevel has finally solved this friction point for individual SaaS accounts. When you enable SaaS for a sub-account and attach a snapshot, the platform now checks for conflicts against existing assets before making any destructive moves.

### How the New Safeguard Works

Instead of a blind overwrite, the system analyzes the target sub-account. If it detects that a snapshot asset overlaps with something already customized by the client, it halts the process and surfaces a warning modal.

From there, you or your client have agency-level control. You can choose to preserve the existing local customization or force-overwrite it with the incoming snapshot version. If there is zero conflict, the deployment finishes silently in the background just like it always did.

For bulk SaaS onboarding, HighLevel is taking a conservative route: asset protection is applied automatically by default, ensuring no client work gets accidentally torched during massive batch setups.

### Practical Implications for Agency Operators

This update changes how you should think about client management and template updates:

1. **Confident Iteration:** You can now push global improvements to your master snapshot without living in fear that you will break live client modifications.
2. **Better Client Trust:** Clients who like to tinker with their own pages will no longer lose their work when you push down structural updates from your agency level.
3. **Safer SaaS Scaling:** As you lean harder into automated onboarding, having a built-in conflict resolver dramatically cuts down on support tickets caused by rogue deployments.

If you have been holding back on automating your client onboarding because template updates felt too risky, this fix removes the primary hazard. It is a welcome quality-of-life update for any team running [HighLevel SaaS mode](https://www.gohighlevel.com/protrial?fp_ref=affiliatealfa) at scale.
