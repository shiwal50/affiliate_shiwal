If you run an agency with multiple sales reps or distinct onboarding tracks, you have likely run into the limitations of HighLevel's traditional random split action. Setting an even percentage split on a random configuration never quite delivered true 50/50 groups—especially with smaller lead volumes. Contacts would bunch up, and teams would end up unevenly loaded.

HighLevel has rolled out a much-needed update to the workflow builder by introducing Even and Weighted split distribution types. This gives agencies precise, predictable control over how leads move through automated paths.

### Breaking Down the New Distribution Types

Previously, your main choice for dividing traffic was a percentage-based random assignment. The new options completely change how you can structure multi-path automations:

* **Even Split:** This operates on a strict turn-based rotation. If you have two paths, every incoming contact alternates seamlessly. Lead one goes left, lead two goes right, lead three goes left. It ensures absolute balance without relying on mathematical probability.
* **Weighted Split:** This lets you dictate specific percentages using whole numbers that total 100%. For instance, a 70/30 split will deliberately route seven out of every ten contacts down your primary path and three down the secondary path. 

### Real-World Agency Use Cases

For marketing agencies managing client accounts, this update solves several common operational bottlenecks:

1. **Balanced Sales Team Rotation:** When distributing inbound leads across a sales team of two or three closers, an Even split guarantees that account managers receive an identical volume of opportunities over time, preventing internal friction over lead cherry-picking.
2. **Capacity-Based Routing:** If you run a high-ticket service alongside a low-ticket automated product, a Weighted split allows you to direct 80% of warm prospects to your primary setter workflow while siphoning 20% into an automated nurture sequence. This matches lead flow directly to team bandwidth.
3. **Controlled A/B Testing:** When testing two different email follow-up sequences, Even splits ensure your sample sizes remain identical day in and day out, giving you statistically significant data much faster.

### How to Set It Up in Your Sub-Accounts

Configuring the new split is straightforward. Open any automation workflow, add or edit a Split action, and look at the Distribution Type dropdown. Select either Even split or Weighted split, label your paths clearly, and save. 

Keep in mind that once contacts enter these split paths, the distribution settings lock in to protect your data integrity. If you need to reconfigure the percentages later, you will need to use the reset test feature, which clears previous statistics and restarts the rotation.
