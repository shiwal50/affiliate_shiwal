If you manage multiple sub-accounts for clients, you know the frustration of the one-size-fits-all agency dashboard. Historically, whenever a client or a team member opened a sub-account, they were greeted by macro-level metrics that lumped everyone's data together. If a specific sales rep only wanted to see their own closed deals, or a client wanted a high-level view filtered strictly by a single lead source, you had to rely on manual filtering during every session or build complicated workarounds. HighLevel has finally addressed this bottleneck by rolling out full configuration and filtering capabilities for general widgets on sub-account dashboards.

### What Changed in Sub-Account Dashboards

HighLevel now allows agency operators to edit general dashboard widgets directly. Instead of displaying a blanket overview of all CRM activity, you can lock widgets down using a dedicated Conditions tab. You can filter data permanently by assigned users, specific tags, lead sources, custom fields, and attribution models. 

Beyond basic filtering, the update introduces date range overrides. You can now pin a widget to a specific time frame—like 'This Year' compared against 'Last Year'—independently of the global dashboard date filter. Crucially, the widget face itself now clearly displays the active date property and range, eliminating confusion when two similar metrics show different totals.

For agencies running white-label environments or building client portals, these updates make it drastically easier to transform a generic reporting screen into a clean, client-ready display. You can rename widgets to match client-friendly terminology, apply visual themes that match your branding, and duplicate widgets to segment data cleanly.

### Practical Implications for Agency Operations

Think about how this changes client onboarding and internal accountability:

1. **Role-Based Views Without Complexity:** By setting the assigned user condition to the logged-in user permanently, sales reps automatically see only their own performance metrics the second they log in. No extra clicks required.
2. **Eliminating Manual Reporting:** If a client only cares about enterprise-tier leads originating from specific ad campaigns, you can segment that data once via tags or source filters and leave it locked in place. 
3. **Visual Clarity:** Year-over-year growth stories can now be baked directly into the primary view, giving stakeholders immediate visibility into long-term trends without requiring custom report builders.

### How to Set It Up

Configuring your widgets takes just a few steps:

* Navigate to the target sub-account dashboard and click **Edit dashboard** in the top right corner.
* Hover over any general widget, click the three-dot menu, and select **Edit**.
* Use the **Configure** tab to update titles or apply date range overrides under Advanced Settings.
* Jump to the **Conditions** tab to assign permanent field, operator, and value filters.
* Apply your preferred visual theme and hit **Save**.

These persistent filters remain locked in place every time the dashboard loads, saving your team from redundant configuration work.
