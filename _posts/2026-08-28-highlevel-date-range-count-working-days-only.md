If you run appointment-driven workflows or client booking pages for businesses that keep standard operating hours—or custom schedules like Tuesday-through-Thursday—you've likely dealt with the awkward friction of calendar date ranges swallowing up weekends. Previously, setting a rolling booking window of a few days meant that if a client checked your calendar late in the week, the active booking window would silently bleed into Saturday and Sunday. The result? A booking widget that looked deceptively empty or forced prospects to look at dates when the business was actually locked up.

HighLevel has rolled out a much-needed fix across Calendars, Services, and Rentals with a new toggle: **Count available days only**. 

### How the Working Days Toggle Works

When you flip this new setting on beneath your standard Date Range configuration, HighLevel's scheduling engine stops counting dead calendar days. Instead, it looks directly at your availability schedule and skips weekends or days marked as closed. 

For example, if a local service business is closed on Saturdays and Sundays and uses a tight two-day booking window, a visitor landing on the page on a Friday afternoon used to see a broken or confusing view. With the new toggle enabled, the system cleanly extends the window to the next active working days—Monday and Tuesday. It treats your actual operational calendar as the source of truth rather than blindly counting 48 hours off the clock.

This also plays nicely with minimum scheduling notice periods. The notice rule clears out the required lead time first, and then the date range calculates the exact number of active working days following that buffer. 

### Why Agency Builders Should Care

For marketing agencies managing local service providers, clinics, salons, or contractors, calendar configuration details dictate conversion rates. When booking widgets show dead space or throw off confusing availability loops, prospects drop off. Support tickets pile up from clients wondering why their customers can't book specific slots.

Because this update applies across standard meeting calendars, round-robin setups, collective scheduling, and service or rental listings, you can standardize how availability behaves across your entire sub-account portfolio. Date-specific hour overrides are also respected automatically—if a business blocks off a holiday or adds an ad-hoc Saturday shift, the counting logic adapts on the fly.

If you are building out automated client onboarding templates or auditing your existing booking funnels, take a few minutes to review your calendar settings. Turning on this single toggle eliminates edge cases where weekend closures break high-intent lead generation.
