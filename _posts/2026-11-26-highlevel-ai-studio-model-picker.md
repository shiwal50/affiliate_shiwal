If you have been building complex assets inside HighLevel's AI Studio, you have likely run into a frustrating limitation: treating every prompt with the exact same underlying model. Whether you were drafting a minor one-line copy tweak or generating a heavy, multi-page structure, the system relied on a uniform approach. That meant you either overpaid in compute time for simple edits or lacked the absolute heaviest horsepower when tackling intricate structural builds.

HighLevel has just eliminated that bottleneck by rolling out a manual model picker for AI Studio. Now, agencies operating on qualifying plans can explicitly choose which model powers each specific message. Instead of guessing blindly, you can hand off difficult logic and multi-step builds to heavy-hitting models, while delegating quick formatting tasks to faster, leaner options.

### What Changed in the AI Studio Composer

When you open a project or work from the dashboard, you will notice a new Auto · Beta chip sitting right beneath the chat box. Clicking this opens a grouped menu of available models. At launch, the lineup includes prominent options from major families like Claude, GPT, and Gemini—featuring specific variants optimized for different types of execution.

If you prefer a hands-off approach, you do not need to change a thing. Auto remains the default setting, letting the system choose the best fit on the fly. But when you need granular control, selecting a specific model changes how that individual message is processed. Your selection stays remembered for the project, and every finished turn clearly labels which engine did the heavy lifting. This kind of transparency helps agency teams audit their workflows and figure out which configurations yield the cleanest outputs.

### Practical Implications for Agency Builders

For marketing agencies deploying custom workflows, funnels, and automated assets for clients, this update introduces real cost and quality management. Complex logic requires deep reasoning capabilities. By assigning a top-tier model like Claude Opus or a robust GPT variant to complex architectural tasks, you reduce the iteration cycles needed to get a build right the first time.

Conversely, when you are iterating rapidly on tone adjustments, localized text styling, or short-form email sequences, dropping down to a faster, lighter model preserves speed and cuts down on resource burn. Because billing adjusts based on the specific model utilized per message, you have direct leverage over your margin and resource allocation.

If you want to see how these advanced capabilities fit into a broader agency automation stack, take a closer look at [HighLevel's suite of AI tools](https://www.gohighlevel.com/ai?fp_ref=affiliatealfa) to understand how native generation tools are evolving.

### Getting Started with the Model Picker

Access to the manual picker is currently rolling out for agencies on the Founder Plan and sub-accounts on the AI Employee plan. If your account qualifies, navigate to your sub-account dashboard, open AI Studio, and click the Auto chip to inspect the available engines. 

Stop letting basic copy tweaks and complex architecture share the exact same computational weight. Match your tools to the complexity of the task, keep an eye on your execution logs, and fine-tune your agency's build pipeline for maximum efficiency.
