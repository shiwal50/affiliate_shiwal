If you manage multiple sub-accounts for clients, you know the frustration of the one-size-fits-all agency dashboard. Until now, general widgets in HighLevel sub-accounts showed a macro view of data that required manual sorting or left team members looking at metrics irrelevant to their day-to-day responsibilities. HighLevel has rolled out a major update that finally makes general widgets on sub-account dashboards fully editable, filterable, and brandable.

### What Changed in Sub-Account Dashboards

Agency owners and client stakeholders can now configure what individual widgets display using permanent filter conditions, date range overrides, visual themes, and custom naming. Instead of relying purely on global dashboard filters, you can lock specific widgets down to exact parameters.

The new Conditions tab lets you scope a widget by assigned user, tags, source, lead value, custom fields, and attribution data. Once saved, these conditions persist every time the dashboard opens. 

### Practical Agency Use Cases

**1. The "Logged-In User" View for Sales Reps**
In a busy sales environment, reps don't need to see the entire team's pipeline metrics every time they log in. By setting a permanent condition where `Assigned user = Logged in user`, every individual team member automatically sees only their own performance metrics without needing to toggle filters manually during every single session.

**2. Year-Over-Year Growth Tracking for Clients**
Clients love seeing long-term growth, but dashboard date filters often default to monthly or quarterly views. You can now override the date range on a specific widget to lock it to *This Year* while enabling comparison mode against *Last Year*. This builds a permanent growth story directly into the client's home screen.

**3. Segment-Specific Metrics Without Duplicate Pipelines**
If you want to track a specific lead source or a custom field tag—such as high-value enterprise leads versus standard inquiries—you can duplicate a general widget, apply a permanent tag filter, and rename the widget title to reflect that exact segment. 

### Client-Ready Customization

Beyond raw data manipulation, this update helps agencies deliver a more polished, white-label experience. You can rename generic widget titles into client-friendly language, apply visual themes that match your branding or the client's brand guidelines, and ensure that the date property and active date range are explicitly visible on the widget face. This transparency cuts down on confusion when stakeholders compare different reports.

Before rolling these changes out across all client accounts, test them in a staging sub-account. Set up your base templates, apply your preferred widget themes, and verify your custom field conditions.
