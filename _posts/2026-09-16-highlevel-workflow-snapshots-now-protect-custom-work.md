If you run an agency on HighLevel, you know the quiet panic of pushing a global snapshot update to a client sub-account. You want to roll out a new funnel or workflow template, but you also know there is a solid chance the client—or your own team—manually tweaked assets in that specific sub-account. Previously, applying a snapshot meant holding your breath and hoping it wouldn't silently overwrite those custom changes.

HighLevel has finally fixed this blind spot with a critical update that protects your sub-account customizations when applying snapshots.

The real-world agency implication here is massive, especially for teams running [HighLevel SaaS mode](https://www.gohighlevel.com/protrial?fp_ref=affiliatealfa) where client customization is a regular occurrence. When you enable SaaS for an account and attach a snapshot, the system now runs a proactive conflict check. If the incoming snapshot touches an asset that has already been locally modified, it stops, throws a clear warning, and lets you choose: keep the local work or overwrite it with the master version.

### Why This Changes Client Management

Think about how sub-account management usually breaks down at scale:

1. **The Customization Trap:** A local client tweaks their landing page copy or adds a bespoke form to handle a unique local offer.
2. **The Global Push:** Your agency pushes a routine system-wide snapshot update to deploy a new automation sequence.
3. **The Silent Wipe:** The snapshot overwrites the local landing page, erasing the client's custom work without warning.
4. **The Cleanup:** You spend an hour on support trying to reconstruct what broke.

Now, that entire friction point is removed. You get granular control on an individual SaaS account level (covering both SaaS V1 and V2), ensuring that local wins aren't accidentally destroyed by global rollouts. For bulk SaaS enablement, the system currently applies an automatic safeguard by default—protecting existing assets without prompting—while full individual control brings much-needed transparency to your deployment pipeline.

### How to Leverage This in Your Agency

This update lets agencies push standardized templates with total confidence. You no longer have to maintain separate, fragile snapshot variants just because one client made minor tweaks to a default funnel. You can push updates safely, review conflicts on the spot, and preserve local goodwill with your clients.

If you have been hesitant to aggressively push global updates down to active sub-accounts because of collateral damage fears, this update removes the risk. It is a welcome quality-of-life upgrade for any team managing dozens or hundreds of client environments.
