Agency clients have a bad habit of staring at metrics that don't concern them. When a local business owner logs into their sub-account and sees company-wide pipeline totals, unassigned tasks, or lead metrics generated by other team members, it creates unnecessary friction and endless support questions. 

HighLevel has finally solved this issue by rolling out editable and filterable general widgets for sub-account dashboards. This update lets you tailor what individual users see down to the specific field, eliminating the need to build separate, custom dashboards from scratch for every single client.

### What You Can Now Control

Previously, dashboard widgets were rigid. They pulled global sub-account data by default, forcing everyone to look at the exact same high-level overview regardless of their role. 

The new configuration suite introduces three core tabs when you edit a dashboard:

1. **The Conditions Tab:** This is the most powerful addition. You can now apply permanent filter conditions based on assigned users, tags, sources, lead values, and custom fields. For example, setting the 'Assigned User' filter to 'Logged in User' ensures that sales reps only see their own pipeline performance the moment they log in. 
2. **Date Range Overrides:** You are no longer bound solely by the global dashboard date filter. You can lock specific widgets to custom timeframes—like year-to-date or last month—and even toggle comparison modes to show performance against previous periods side-by-side.
3. **The Themes Tab:** White-labeling goes deeper than just logos and colors. You can now rename widget titles to match your client's internal vocabulary, apply distinct visual themes, and duplicate modules to isolate specific geographic regions or product categories.

### Why This Matters for Client Retention

Client onboarding is all about perceived value and simplicity. If a client logs in and feels overwhelmed by raw, unfiltered CRM data, they pull away. By using these granular filters, you can present a clean, highly relevant growth story every time they open their portal.

Imagine onboarding a multi-location franchise client. Instead of building a complex labyrinth of separate sub-accounts, you can deploy a single master sub-account and use permanent widget filters to show Location A's manager only their specific metrics, while Location B's manager sees theirs. It keeps data secure, keeps workflows organized, and dramatically cuts down on custom reporting overhead.

### How to Implement It Today

Head over to any sub-account dashboard, hit **Edit Dashboard** in the top right corner, and click the three-dot menu on any general widget. From there, you can layer on your permanent filters, lock down your date ranges, and rename the module to something your clients will instantly understand.

It is a subtle update, but one that removes a major friction point in client-facing dashboards. Set it up once during client onboarding, and let your platform do the heavy lifting from then on.
