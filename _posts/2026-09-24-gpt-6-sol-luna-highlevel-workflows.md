HighLevel has officially rolled out OpenAI's newest language models—GPT-6 Sol and GPT-6 Luna—directly into the AI Agent and GPT actions inside workflows. If you rely on automated triggers, lead scoring, or multi-step conversation logic to keep your agency running, this update cuts operational costs while noticeably tightening the quality of your system outputs.

### What Changed Under the Hood

These new iterations replace the older GPT-5.6 architecture across the board. For agency builders, the practical differences between the two models dictate how you should deploy them across your sub-accounts:

*   **GPT-6 Sol:** Built strictly for complex, multi-step agent actions. Whether you are orchestrating workflows that span multiple external applications or making intricate routing decisions based on fuzzy lead inputs, Sol cuts factual errors down by roughly half compared to its predecessor.
*   **GPT-6 Luna:** Engineered for speed and high-volume operations. If your sub-accounts handle heavy tagging, automated chat summaries, data classification, or rapid initial replies, Luna processes those requests quickly. At higher effort settings, it matches the factual accuracy of older Sol models while operating at a fraction of the budget.

### Real-World Agency Implications

For most marketing agencies, running automated AI steps at scale always comes down to unit economics and reliability. When an AI agent hallucinates a tag or misroutes a prospect, your team spends billable hours untangling the mess. By dropping the error rate on complex reasoning tasks, Sol minimizes the downstream human cleanup required for client onboarding and automated support chains.

More importantly, OpenAI and HighLevel have priced these models at roughly 50 percent lower than the previous generation. GPT-6 Luna in particular delivers savings up to 58 percent per task depending on your configuration. 

If you have hesitated to turn on heavy AI workflows because of API token costs eating into your client retainer margins, this update removes that barrier. You can now build deeper, more conversational automation sequences without worrying about runaway execution fees.

### How to Implement It Today

Getting started takes seconds. Open any existing workflow, drop in or select an active AI Agent or GPT action, and click the Model dropdown. Switch your heavy decision-making steps to GPT-6 Sol and your high-volume categorization tasks to GPT-6 Luna, then save and publish.
